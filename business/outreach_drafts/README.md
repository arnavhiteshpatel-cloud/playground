## Pre-send checklist (all must be true)
- [ ] The founder has decided how to describe the project's independence and IP position, after reading `outreach/IP_AND_OTM_QUESTIONS.md` and, ideally, speaking to UIUC OTM. The standard IP paragraph from `outreach/OUTREACH_EMAILS.md` is included or deliberately left out.
- [ ] The recipient is a **role** reached through the organisation's **public, company-level** route in `../prospects.csv` (contact form, general mailbox on its own site, or LinkedIn company page). No personal email addresses are guessed or scraped.
- [ ] The route was re-checked on the organisation's own site on the day of sending.
- [ ] Every `[…]` placeholder is filled with a fact from `../prospects.csv`, with its source URL, or the sentence is deleted. No invented facts about the recipient.
- [ ] No sentence claims validation on real graft material, accuracy, GLP or Part 11 compliance, regulatory acceptance, customers, pilots, partners, funding or incorporation.
- [ ] No prices appear in a first message. Prices are discussed only in an interview (`../PRICING_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`).
- [ ] No algorithm details are disclosed.
- [ ] At most one follow-up, sent no sooner than 7 days later. Stop at any "no" or on silence after the follow-up. Opt-out wording is included.
- [ ] The contact is logged (date, route, prospect ID, no personal data) in the founder's private tracker.

## Shared placeholders
`[Founder name]`, `[Year/Major]`, `[project email]`, `[link to one-page summary]`, `[prospect fact + source]`.

## Language rules
- Say "research prototype", "being tested", "not yet validated on commercial graft materials".
- Do not say "validated", "GLP-ready", "compliant", "AI-powered", "proven", "customers", "partners".
