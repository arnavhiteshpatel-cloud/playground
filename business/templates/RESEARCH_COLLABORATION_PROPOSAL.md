**Title:** Multi-site validation of semi-automated three-class micro-CT segmentation (new bone / residual graft / host bone) in preclinical bone-defect models
**Partner:** [Institution / core / lab]  **Date:** [ ]

## 1. Rationale
Quantifying bone regeneration in graft-filled defects by micro-CT is complicated by graft materials whose radiodensity overlaps with mineralized tissue. Threshold-based approaches vary between analysts and sites [INF; to be supported with literature in the final version]. A validated, transparent three-class segmentation with human review could improve reproducibility and save analyst time [HYP].

## 2. Aims
1. **Aim 1, agreement:** Compare the OsteoQuant workflow against expert manual analysis on retrospective scans from ≥2 defect models (e.g. rat calvarial, rabbit femoral condyle) and ≥2 graft classes (e.g. CaP ceramic, bioactive glass, xenograft). Endpoints: ICC(2,1), Bland–Altman bias/LoA, Dice per class.
2. **Aim 2, histology concordance:** In a subset with matched histology, correlate micro-CT new-bone and residual-graft fractions with histomorphometry.
3. **Aim 3, efficiency and reproducibility:** Hands-on analyst time, and inter-site reproducibility on a shared scan set.
