# Review cycle 1: imaging physics and biostatistics

Scope: Generation-2 measurement-support advisor (`osteoquant/advisor/`), its pipeline integration, the science docs
(`MEASUREMENT_SUPPORT.md`, `ENDPOINT_DEFINITIONS.md`, `PREREGISTRATION.md`), experiments S1, S1b, F2, L3, U1, the
longitudinal v2 functions and generator v2 `displace_and_fragment`.

Reviewer perspectives: (A) micro-CT imaging physics (IMG) and (B) biostatistics / validation design (STAT).
All checks were short reads or small Python runs on the working tree as of 2026-11-27, about 21:40 UTC. Line
numbers refer to that tree. `separability.py` and `longitudinal/core.py` changed while the review was running:
the single-threshold fix in `dice_oracle_samples` and the peri-defect shell in `registration_qc`. The findings
below take those changes into account. Where a finding applies to results produced before the change, it says so.

Severity: **critical** means the product can report a confident, wrong customer-facing number. **High** means a
validation claim is not supported, or a decision rule is wrong in a reachable configuration. **Medium** means a
bias or design weakness that should be fixed before real-data evaluation. **Low** means a documentation or
consistency problem.
