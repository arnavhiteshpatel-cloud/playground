# Review cycle 1: ML science and software architecture

Scope: `osteoquant/ml/{train,unet,synth_dr}.py`, `experiments/{exp_S2_sim2real,real_dog,exp_U1_ensemble_conformal}.py`,
`osteoquant/pipeline.py`, `osteoquant/advisor/`, with `FORENSIC_AUDIT.md`, `EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md`, `MODEL_REGISTRY.md`,
`ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md` as baseline. Reviewed 2026-11-27 while S2 stage a/b and the Gen-0 reproduction were still running
(no S2b/S2c/U1 result JSON existed yet; `exp_S2ab.log` showed only `gen0_synth_zeroshot zoom 0.5 mean Dice 0.325`).

Checks run for this review (read-only, under 2 CPU-min in total):
- 40 DR samples from 6 training fraction volumes: output shapes, mineral fraction, normalised soft-tissue level.
- `normalize_image` on 2 dog **training** specimens (test specimens not inspected): normalised soft tissue in band vs bone.
- Regenerated `test_001` with generator v2.0.0 and compared with the stored v1.0.0 file: labels and image identical.
- Few-shot subset draws for seeds 0 to 2: no repeated subsets (k=1: [7], [3], [4]; k=3: [1,6,7], [2,6,8], [1,3,5]).

Severity: **Critical** = invalidates a headline claim or the licence guarantee; **High** = biases a reported comparison or can
silently produce wrong product output; **Medium** = weakens evidence or maintainability; **Low** = hygiene.

## What is sound

- The DR fraction volumes come only from the synthetic **training** split, so there is no synthetic test leakage. Labels and intensities in DR are derived from the same zoomed fraction map, so they are consistent.
- The few-shot design is paired (the same subset per seed across arms) and the subsets are distinct across seeds.
- `load_model` checks the name against an allowlist, verifies the hash before deserialising, and uses `weights_only=True`.
- The conformal quantile index `ceil((n+1)(1-alpha))-1` is correct. The cal, val and test seeds are disjoint (5000+, 2000+, 3000+).
- Unsupported endpoints fail closed in `apply_support`: a missing status is treated as `not_supported`.

## Three most valuable additional experiments (4 CPU, no GPU)

1. **Normalisation-matched DR (tests ML-03, ML-07, ML-08, ML-04).** Retrain DR with (a) phantom-level synthesis followed by `normalize_image` and then cropping, (b) an air/outside-specimen level, and (c) isotropic zoom. Use 2 seeds x 1500 iterations (about the cost of the current S2b, run twice). Select test-time zoom on the 10 dog training specimens, then evaluate the 5 test specimens once. Report band Dice and false-positive volume outside the band. This one experiment shows whether the DR result is limited by the method or by the pipeline.
2. **Fair, paired few-shot curve with a larger test pool (tests ML-01, ML-02, ML-05, ML-09).** Arms: scratch, Gen-0 (bone mapped to host, or a 2-class head), DR, Gen-0 as in I; k in {1, 3}; 3 seeds. Use the same loss, n_classes, LR grid {1e-3, 3e-3} (chosen on held-out training specimens) and 300 iterations, plus scratch at 1800. Evaluate on the 5 official test specimens and the 10-k unused training specimens. Analyse paired differences per specimen with a hierarchical bootstrap and a sign-flip test. Cost is about 24 x 300 iterations, comparable to the planned S2c.
3. **U1 conformal and failure-detection re-analysis (tests ML-11, ML-12, ML-13); no training.** Reuse the cached U1 rows: 200 random re-splits of the 56 ID scans into cal and test, gate-recalibrated q (selective conformal), Clopper-Pearson coverage intervals, per-material conditional coverage on `ood_mat`, and failure AUROC stratified within ID and within OOD. Runs in minutes and determines whether the "gated coverage" claim can be stated.
