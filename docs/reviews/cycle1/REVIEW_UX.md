# OsteoQuant Desktop: UX review, cycle 1

**Scope:** `osteoquant/desktop/` (PySide6 + VTK), `docs/desktop/USER_GUIDE.md`, report output as seen from the desktop app.
**Reviewer roles:**
- (A) a preclinical micro-CT analyst who uses CTAn, Dragonfly or 3D Slicer daily;
- (S) a study director who signs off reports.

**Date:** 2026-11-27.

## Method

I drove the application programmatically with `docs/reviews/cycle1/ux_screens/drive_ux.py` (run with `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen` at `nice 10`). Modal message boxes were intercepted and logged. The run covered these steps:

1. Created a project with an admin/analyst and a reviewer account, and a blinded study with two groups.
2. Imported 5 NIfTI phantoms.
3. Imported a second batch of 4 items:
   - a NumPy file with no spacing;
   - a corrupt `.nii.gz`;
   - a TIFF slice folder with no spacing (a duplicate of the NumPy file);
   - a `notes.txt` file.
4. Analysed each scan with a different material: *Unspecified*, *No graft*, HA, collagen sponge, and beta-TCP with the installed U-Net ONNX model.
5. Viewed, painted, undid and redid edits, and saved revision v2.
6. Tried to approve as the author (refused). Signed in as the reviewer, turned on blinded mode, approved v2, generated a report, previewed it and tried to approve it.
7. Verified the audit chain and opened the licence and About dialogs.
8. Cancelled a job, generated a report for an empty study, and checked the dark theme and a 1280×760 window.

All screenshots are 1600×1000 unless the name says otherwise. They are in `docs/reviews/cycle1/ux_screens/`. Inspected data (Results rows, tooltips, job states, messages) is in `ux_screens/ux_log.json`.

Supporting artefacts:
- `ux_report_sample.pdf`: the generated report;
- `cvd_simulate.py` → `ux43`/`ux44`: colour-vision simulations (Machado 2009);
- `theme_crash_repro.py`: the theme-switch crash probe.

`--selftest` passes (8/8 steps, 8.7 s).

**Severity scale:**
- **High**: can mislead a sign-off or block a core analyst workflow.
- **Medium**: noticeable friction or a risk with a workaround.
- **Low**: polish.

Line numbers refer to the files as they were during this review.

## What works well

- **Spacing gate.** Voxel spacing is mandatory: *MISSING* or *ASSUMED* spacing blocks the wizard until it is confirmed (but see UX-22).
- **Rejected imports.** Rejected imports are listed in the tree with reasons. Duplicates are detected by content hash across formats.
- **Separation of duties.** Separation of duties, password re-authentication and the signature meaning are enforced. Failed self-approvals are audited.
- **Audit trail.** Audit-chain verification is one click and gives a clear result.
- **QC split.** QC is split into deterministic and heuristic checks, which is honest labelling.
- **PDF disclosures.** The PDF carries clear "unreviewed", "synthetic-trained model" and "research use" banners, and an evaluation watermark on every page (6/6 pages checked).
- **Themes.** Light and dark themes are consistent. Image viewports stay dark in both themes.
- **Branding and privacy.** There is no gratuitous branding, and the privacy statement is clear.
