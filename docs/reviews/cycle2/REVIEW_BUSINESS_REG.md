# Review cycle 2: business, regulatory, IP/licensing and customer-facing science

**Date:** 2026-11-27. **Reviewer stance:** independent of the authors. Perspectives: CRO operations buyer, quality and
regulatory reviewer (GLP / 21 CFR 58 / Part 11 awareness; nothing may claim compliance), seed investor, IP/licensing
counsel perspective (**not legal advice**), and a bone-biology check of customer-facing science statements.

**Scope.** Cycle-1 reviews `REVIEW_BIO_CRO_QUALITY.md`, `REVIEW_INVESTOR_IP.md` and `RESPONSE_PART2.md`; current
`website/` (all nine pages and `website/screens/*.png`), `docs/user/`, `docs/quality/`, `docs/ip/`, `business/`
(REVENUE_MODEL, templates, financial-model JSON and workbook), `investor/` (FUNDING_STRATEGY, one-pager,
`build_decks.py`), `grants/`, `validation/`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`, `docs/desktop/PACKAGING.md` and the built Linux bundle
in `packaging/dist/OsteoQuant/`, `reports_samples/`, and the code paths behind the report
(`osteoquant/api/bundle.py`, `osteoquant/report/defectfill.py`, `osteoquant/advisor/separability.py`).

**Method.** Read-only, except this file. Checks that were run:
- `python3 scripts/check_claims.py`.
- A fresh DefectFill report, generated in a scratch folder outside the repository through
  `osteoquant.desktop.demo.build_demo_project` and `osteoquant.desktop.services.generate_report`. The input was a
  synthetic β-TCP condyle demo with five specimens, and the output was read with `pdftotext`.
- `assess_support()` (advisor 1.3.2) at the website's illustrative setting: noise 0.08, 20 µm voxels, graft share
  0.3, healing time unset and 2 weeks.
- The result JSONs (`exp_S1*`, `exp_L3`) compared with the numbers quoted in the documents.
- `financial_model_outputs.json`, and the workbook opened with cached values.
- The bundle's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`, `licenses/INDEX.json`, and the shared-library list.
- `git ls-files`, and the branch's tracking state. The branch has no unpushed commits.

**Severity** follows cycle 1:
- **Critical**: a sponsor- or investor-facing claim is wrong as delivered, or there is a live legal exposure.
- **High**: it blocks a pilot, external sharing or diligence, or misleads by omission.
- **Medium**: a weakness to fix before outreach.
- **Low**: hygiene.

## Verdict

The cycle-1 remediation fixed the most important report defect: the PDF now prints support status, conditions,
"n/m" and the evidence stage. It also re-baselined the quality documents to the desktop product and removed the
GPL-only Qt module. The new website and user documentation are, in tone, unusually careful. Every page carries the
research-product banner, synthetic and real results are separated, and the services page lists what is not sold.

Three problems remain:

1. **The customer-facing science describes an advisor that no longer exists.** The website and user documents cite
   advisor 1.2.1, but the shipped code and the PDF it writes say 1.3.2. The per-material table promises support that
   1.3.2 refuses, and the evidence numbers quoted no longer match the result files. The claims checker does not
   cover any of them (C2-BIZ-01 to -03).
2. **The investor and financial artefacts are unchanged since cycle 1.** The one-pager still says "61 tests",
   "verified", "independent review" and "audit-ready". The model still books grants as operating cash and 2027
   revenue before its prerequisites exist. The decks are still Gen-1 (C2-BIZ-11, -12).
3. **Licence and IP housekeeping is half done.**
   - Licence: the Qt texts now ship, but the system libraries, Qt's own third-party notices, the PSF licence and
     PDFium have no notices. The SBOM the website points to is stale.
   - IP: the IP strategy body still plans trade secrets for material that is public, and every invention in the
     repository is now public with a single US bar date of 2027-11-27 (C2-BIZ-08 to -10 and -22).

No statement found claims GLP or Part 11 compliance. "Audit-ready" (website About page, one-pager) and "validated
service" (grant drafts) are the remaining compliance-flavoured phrases.
