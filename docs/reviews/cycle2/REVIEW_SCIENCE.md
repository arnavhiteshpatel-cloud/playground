# Review cycle 2: science (imaging physics, biostatistics, bone biology, ML)

Scope: the cycle-1 science findings (IMG, STAT, ML, BIO) and their responses; the measurement-support advisor from
1.1.0 to 1.3.3 (`osteoquant/advisor/`), and the 1.4.0 commit (a22da5a) where it changes a conclusion; experiments S1,
S1b, S1c, S1H, L3, L4 and F2; `longitudinal/core.py`; `knowledge/materials.json`; and the support rendering in
`report/defectfill.py`.

Method: read-only review of the tree on 2026-11-28 between 00:10 and 01:00 UTC, while the tree changed (S1 re-run
finished at 00:13, S1H at 00:13, 1.4.0 and S1H2 were committed, S1H2 started running). All advisor checks below use a
frozen export of commit d0c9696 (advisor **1.3.3**), unpacked into a scratch directory, so the 1.4.0 edits in the
working tree do not affect them. Each Python check took under 1 CPU-min. Line numbers refer to HEAD at about 00:55.

Severity uses the cycle-1 scale:
- **Critical:** a confident, wrong customer-facing number.
- **High:** an unsupported validation claim, or a rule that is wrong in a reachable configuration.
- **Medium:** a bias or design weakness to fix before real data.
- **Low:** documentation or consistency.

## Summary

1. **The development result "0 supported-but-failed" is an in-sample (training) error.**
   - Every supported-but-failed case seen during development was removed by a rule written after inspecting it, then
     re-scored on the same rows. That is about 51 events across S1b, S1c and L3 (table in C2-SCI-01).
   - On the first held-out set (S1H, advisor 1.3.3), failures among supported cases were:

     | Endpoint | Failed / supported | Share |
     |---|---|---|
     | Residual graft | 4/16 | 25% |
     | New bone | 10/10 | 100% |
     | Total mineral | 0/47 | 0% |

   - A one-minute re-test of the 1.3.2 control-reference rule with a less mature control animal gave 3/24 graft
     failures. In-sample it was 0/18.
   - Only total mineral, measured with the advisor's own threshold method, held up out of sample.
2. **The support status does not depend on the segmentation method, and validation used a different method from the
   default (critical).**
   - The desktop and pipeline default is multi-Otsu.
   - In the S1 re-run under 1.3.3 with that default, total mineral was "supported" on 50/56 specimens. Four of them
     were wrong by −21% to −25%, and 0/4 of the mineral failures were refused.
   - All the favourable per-endpoint numbers come from the `advisor_threshold` method, which customers do not get by
     default.
3. **Several product rules grant support for things the software does not do or check.**
   - A day-0 "conditional" status comes from a free config dict. No product code computes it, and no product code
     applies the day-0 footprint (C2-SCI-03).
   - Measured reference samples ignore the scan's noise (IMG-03c is not fixed).
   - Metals get "supported" total mineral.
4. **The new registration QC fails at perfect alignment for segmental defects.** The tube geometry creates an
   aperture problem. F2 and B10 ("QC pass 16/16") were produced with the superseded QC and are stale.
5. **The report misrepresents group statistics when endpoints are partly suppressed.** The group n counts all animals.
   A test that could not be computed is printed with a numeric Holm p of 1.000.
6. **Pre-registration weaknesses.**
   - The acceptance rule waives the P(fail | supported) bound when fewer than 30 cases are supported. Under it, S1H
     graft "passes" with 4/16 supported failures.
   - The deviations log has no entry for advisor 1.2.0 through 1.4.0.
   - S1H2, now running, keeps the simulator's structural assumptions (Gaussian PSF, exact material profile, the same
     woven-bone model). Section (c) proposes a held-out test that varies them. It costs about 40 CPU-min.

**Is P(fail | supported) the right acceptance quantity?** It is the right customer-facing quantity: the chance that a
value printed as supported is wrong. It has four limits:

1. In a designed grid it depends on the mix of conditions, so it does not transfer to a customer's case mix. It must be
   reported per stratum (defect model × material class × timepoint) and judged on the worst stratum.
2. It must be counted over independent units (structures with independently drawn conditions), not rows.
3. Dichotomising at 20% hides magnitude. The advisor's own reason text says "within ±10%", yet supported errors
   reached 13.1% (S1b Part A), 11.7% (S1c) and 27.9% (S1H). An upper tolerance bound on |error| among supported cases
   should be co-primary.
4. It must be paired with coverage (P(supported | accurate)) and use the CP lower bound for sensitivity (C2-SCI-16).

## (c) Proposed held-out validation within the simulator (S1H3)

**Purpose.** Test the frozen advisor, 1.4.0 or later, on factors that no version was tuned or diagnosed on. These are
structural misspecifications of the advisor's own assumptions, not just new parameter values. S1 to S1H are all
development data for 1.4.0, so S1H3 must also avoid S1H's and S1H2's settings.

**Firewall.**
1. Pre-register the full script, the analysis code and the acceptance criteria in git before any data are generated.
2. Record the advisor commit SHA and the module hash in the results. The script asserts both.
3. Derive seeds by commit-reveal: commit SHA-256(salt) now, publish the salt at run time, and set seed_i =
   H(salt ∥ i). No seed can then have been seen during tuning.
4. Nobody tunes on S1H3. A failure produces a new version and needs a new held-out set (S1H4).

**Unit and design.**
- The unit is the **structure**: one phantom with its own seed.
- Each structure gets 3 condition draws by Latin-hypercube sampling over the factors below. This avoids crossing a
  small number of structures with a full grid (C2-SCI-17).
- Densities are enriched towards the supported region (60% of draws at X ≥ 1600 and σ ≤ 120). This makes about 30–40%
  of draws supported, so each endpoint can reach the 36 independent supported cases needed to show a bound of 0.10.

| Factor | Held-out values (never used in S1b, S1c, S1H or S1H2) | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| Phantom seeds and defect models | 32 new structures per model (condyle, calvarial, segmental) | Independence |
| Granule morphology | Compact 600–900 µm; strut 150–300 µm (roughness 0.4); mixed sizes | Partial volume and thickness estimation, the strut case S1H exposed |
| Graft density X | Continuous 1100–3100 mgHA/cm³ (Latin-hypercube) | Interpolation and extrapolation |
| **Profile misspecification** | Advisor profile = (X/1075)·(1+ε), ε ∈ {−15, −8, 0, +8, +15}% | The mismatch rule and the decision under a wrong profile (the dominant real-world error) |
| **Host anchor** | Host cortical/trabecular mineral scaled to give a host level of 940–1030 instead of about 1075 (`material_overrides`) | IMG-08d / BIO-05 anchor shift |
| **New-bone maturity model** | `woven_mineral` (550, 900) or (700, 1080); healing stage 0.3–0.8; `new_bone_tb_sp_um` 200–450 | The advisor's assumed 0.60–1.00 range no longer matches the simulator |
| **PSF shape** | Non-Gaussian: a core σ of 0.5–0.9 voxel plus a 10–15% tail with σ of 2–3 voxels; or a sharpened kernel with overshoot. Applied in the script before noise, as S1H did for noise | `estimate_blur` and the blur-aware partial-volume model under a mis-specified PSF |
| **Noise** | `poisson_scale` > 0 (signal-dependent), plus correlated noise with an anisotropic kernel | The noise estimate taken from soft tissue only (IMG-08c) |
| **Artefacts** | `cupping` 0.03–0.06; ring amplitude 0.2σ | Host-anchor and threshold drift |
| Voxel size | 40 µm for all; a 20 µm subset (12 structures × 2 draws) | Voxel/PSF interaction |

| Arm D: method | Every Arm A specimen also scored with multi-Otsu | C2-SCI-02 (status validity for the default method) |

**Outcome and analysis.**
- Score per endpoint (residual graft, new bone, total mineral): failure is a relative volume error above 20%, with the
  absolute error in percent of TV reported alongside.
- Report sensitivity with its CP lower bound, and P(fail | supported) with its CP upper bound, per endpoint, per arm
  and per stratum (defect model × morphology × misspecification level).
- Report a 95/95 upper tolerance bound on |error| among supported cases.
- Fit a logistic GEE (structure as cluster) of failure among supported cases on the factors. This identifies which
  misspecification drives false support.
- Use a cluster bootstrap over structures for every interval.

**Acceptance.** The advisor passes for an endpoint only when all of the following hold:
- the sensitivity lower bound is at least 0.90;
- the P(fail | supported) upper bound is at most 0.10 overall, with n_supported ≥ 36;
- no stratum has more than one supported failure;
- the tolerance bound on |error| is at most 20%.

Otherwise the verdict is "fail" or "not demonstrable". It is never "descriptive pass".

**CPU cost (4 shared CPUs).** Rates measured in this review:
- S1H: 108 evaluations plus 9 phantom generations in 84 s.
- A phantom generation plus one evaluation takes about 5 s at 40 µm.
- 1.4.0 adds local thickness and blur fitting; assume 2 s per evaluation.

| Part | Work | CPU |
|---|---|---|
| Arm A | 96 phantoms × 5 s + 288 evaluations × 2 s | ≈ 18 min |
| Arm B | 24 phantoms + 48 evaluations | ≈ 4 min |
| Arm C | 24 treated + 48 control phantoms + 48 evaluations | ≈ 8 min |
| Arm D | multi-Otsu on Arm A | < 1 min |
| 20 µm subset | 12 phantoms (8× voxels, ≈ 40 s each) + 24 evaluations × 16 s | ≈ 14 min |

Total: about 45 CPU-min, or 15–20 min wall-clock with 3 worker processes. Even at twice the estimate it is under
1.5 CPU-h, which fits alongside the experiment queue. An optional Arm E (day-0 with DBBM-like non-resorbing granules,
surface apposition and motion 0–150 µm; 24 pairs with registration) adds about 20 CPU-min and is needed before
C2-SCI-10 or C2-SCI-03 can be closed.

## Priority order

1. C2-SCI-02, C2-SCI-03 and C2-SCI-14. All three cause wrong or misattributed numbers in the default product and report.
2. C2-SCI-01, C2-SCI-16, C2-SCI-22: honest labelling of development vs held-out evidence, and acceptance rules that
   cannot be passed by rarely supporting.
3. C2-SCI-07, C2-SCI-06, C2-SCI-08, C2-SCI-09: physics gaps that produce supported failures. Then run S1H3.
4. C2-SCI-04, C2-SCI-21, C2-SCI-19, C2-SCI-20: QC and tracking before any day-0 claim. Re-run F2 and L4.
5. C2-SCI-05, C2-SCI-10, C2-SCI-11, C2-SCI-12, C2-SCI-13: rules that systematically refuse correct measurements for
   strut ceramics, DBBM and BCP, or treat materials inconsistently.
