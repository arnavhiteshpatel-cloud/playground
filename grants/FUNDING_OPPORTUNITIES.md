- **URLs:** https://landuyt.illinois.edu/programs/cozad/faq · https://landuyt.illinois.edu/programs/cozad/rules · https://landuyt.illinois.edu/programs/cozad/prizes · https://calendars.illinois.edu/detail/976?eventId=33564262 · https://www.distillerylabs.org/news/20261102

- **IP [V, FAQ]:** "The University and the Landuyt Center do not receive the right to own your IP if you participate". This covers participation only. University ownership under the General Rules can still apply if university facilities are used (§3.17).
- **Prizes, 2026 [V, prizes page]:** TechNexus Grand Prize "$100,000 TechNexus SAFE"; 2nd $50,000 SAFE; 3rd $25,000 SAFE; Pear VC $75k in SAFEs; Multimodal $50k SAFE; Carle Illinois Health Innovation Prizes $25k; Sports Medicine Prize $20k; Microsoft Azure credits $25k per award for AI software startups; Heinz MedTech Prize (Class II or higher device: not applicable). "All of the top cash prizes in Cozad are awarded in the form of SAFE funding." [V FAQ]
- **Track eligibility [V, rules page, 2024–25 framework]:** For Sports Medicine and Healthcare Innovation, "teams must have at least one CI MED student on their team". To compete for health prizes, **recruit a Carle Illinois College of Medicine student** [INF].
- **Rules and deliverables:** The 2027 rule book is not public [UNR]. The 2018–19 Official Rules [V-old] (https://ws.engr.illinois.edu/blogs/getfile/60/31061) included:
  - an 11-item executive-summary form at registration
  - a one-line pitch of about 200 characters
  - a 90-second elevator pitch with one static 16:9 slide
  - a 5-minute final pitch with 3 minutes of Q&A
  - a 5-page business summary and Business Model Canvas
  - a Demo Showcase poster
  - venture exclusion if revenue exceeded $50k or equity raised exceeded $50k
  - "For all teams that win funding greater than $500, in order to receive funding, your team needs to first become a company and then become a vendor of the university."
  - "Non-Confidentiality" of all events.
- **Fit:** 5. No incorporation is needed to compete. It provides mentoring, an IP Clinic pathway, a Research Park/EnterpriseWorks award pathway, and visibility with Illinois Ventures-linked investors.
