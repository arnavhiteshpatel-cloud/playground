# Letter-of-Support Templates (DRAFT)

**Prepared:** 2026-11-27.

**Rules for using these templates:**
- Each letter must be **written or adapted, and signed, by the letter writer** on their own letterhead, describing commitments they actually make. These templates are prompts, not text to sign unchanged.
- Never ask a writer to endorse results that do not exist. Use the **[PENDING RESULT]** convention or omit.
- Check whether the target program accepts letters:
  - **NSF Phase I:** letters of collaboration are typically limited in format [UNR: check NSF 26-510 / PAPPG before drafting]. NSF reviewers value **customer letters**.
  - **NIH:** letters go in "Letters of Support" (PHS 398 Research Plan) [CC].
  - **Cozad / iVenture:** references may be requested ("The application asks questions about your venture, team, progress, and references" [V iVenture]).
- No one has been contacted. Do not send anything without the founder's decision.

Placeholders: `[WRITER NAME]`, `[TITLE]`, `[INSTITUTION/COMPANY]`, `[COMPANY NAME]` (OsteoQuant entity, once formed), `[FOUNDER NAME]`, `[DATE]`.
