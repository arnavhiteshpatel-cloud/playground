# Team and Eligibility Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-11-27. Labels follow FUNDING_OPPORTUNITIES.md. See NSF_vs_NIH_ELIGIBILITY.md for the source quotes.

## 1. The binding constraint

**PI primary employment.** NSF SBIR, NSF STTR and NIH SBIR require the PI to be >50% employed by the company **at award and for the whole project** [V]. NSF treats employment elsewhere above 19.6 h/week as a conflict [V]. The archived NSF FAQ says "anything that prevents an individual from meeting this legal employment requirement (including residency status or university policy)" makes that person ineligible [V-old]. Only **NIH STTR** allows the PD/PI to be primarily employed by the partner research institution [V].

**Second constraint: the company must exist** before any federal full proposal:
- NSF: a legal entity at full proposal [V-old FAQ]; registrations [V].
- NIH: SAM/UEI ("6 weeks or more"), SBA Company Registry and eRA Commons [V].

The NSF Project Pitch needs neither [V].

**Third constraint: IP rights.** The company must have rights to the technology. University ownership may attach to work done with University facilities [CC]. Get an OTM determination early [INF].

## 4. Ownership and program eligibility checks
- **SBIR/STTR:** ≥51% owned and controlled by US citizens or permanent residents; <500 employees [V].
- **iVenture:** "A majority of your team must be University of Illinois students who hold majority ownership." [V] Equity grants to non-students must keep this true during iVenture.
- **Cozad:** ≥1 full-time degree-seeking UIUC student [V]. Historical rules limited non-Illinois members' roles (presenter or team leader must be an Illinois student) [V-old].
- **DCEO match:** Illinois principal place of business; IRS "regarded entity" [V].
- **Illinois Ventures:** UI System connection [V].
