# Founder Prompt — Section Map and the 20 Final Questions

The founder's full mandate ("Project Atlas", Parts I–XV) is condensed for agents in `MANDATE_AND_FOUNDER_BRIEF.md`.
This file preserves the structure of the original prompt and its closing questions verbatim, so that deliverables can
be checked against them.

## Section map of the original prompt
- **Part I — Mission, objectives, strategic philosophy (§1–8):** mission; ultimate objective (first operating company →
  diversified holding company); absolute industry agnosticism; separation from OsteoQuant/Concord (no VC criteria);
  resources ($100k conditional backing, Claude access to 2026-12-05, Illinois/US); $100k is not a spending target;
  financial milestones ($1k/mo → $1M+/yr, analytical not forecasts); success metrics (FCF, ROIC, cash conversion…).
- **Part II — Autonomous research operating system (§9–12):** scale; persistent workspace; independent workstreams;
  authorization boundaries (research and drafts only — no spending, contracts, accounts, contact, or representation).
- **Part III — Exhaustive discovery (§13–16):** 300–500+ distinct opportunities; every major industry; commercial
  mechanisms independent of industries; structural opportunity.
- **Part IV — Evidence-driven screening (§17–22):** opportunity database fields; real operators; purchasing behaviour;
  suppliers; customers; customer acquisition (first, next 10, next 100).
- **Part V — Competition and entry (§23–25).** **Part VI — Financial intelligence (§26–32):** transaction economics;
  revenue vs profit vs cash; capital scenarios; working capital; ROIC; driver-based models; stress tests.
- **Part VII — Wealth creation and compounding (§33–37).** **Part VIII — AI, operations, organization (§38–42).**
- **Part IX — Legal and regulatory feasibility (§43–44).** **Part X — Funnel and ranking (§45–47):** Stages A–H;
  provisional weights (18/15/12/12/10/10/8/5/5/5); alternative rankings.
- **Part XI — Finalist playbooks (§48–49).** **Part XII — Launch (§50–54):** 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year; materials.
- **Part XIII — Evidence quality and adversarial review (§55–61):** A–E hierarchy; source management; analytical
  failure checklist; failed businesses; adversarial review; pre-mortem; constructive skepticism.
- **Part XIV — Final deliverables (§62–73):** master report; opportunity workbook; financial workbooks; evidence,
  supplier and prospect databases; playbooks; capital allocation report; long-term wealth report; independent review
  report; executive decision memo; ChatGPT Deep Research handoff.
- **Part XV — Quality and final instructions (§74–80).**

## The 20 final questions (§79, verbatim)
1. What specific business should I establish?
2. Why is it superior to hundreds of alternatives?
3. What exactly does it sell?
4. Who actually purchases it?
5. Why would customers choose my company?
6. How do I acquire my first customers?
7. How do I obtain the product or fulfill the service?
8. What does a representative transaction earn?
9. How much money do I actually need to start?
10. How can I validate the business before significant expenditure?
11. What are the most dangerous assumptions?
12. What could cause the business to fail?
13. What do I personally need to learn and do?
14. What can Claude automate or materially improve?
15. How does the business become more profitable as it grows?
16. When should I hire employees?
17. How can daily operations eventually be delegated?
18. How much capital can the business realistically generate?
19. When could it begin financing other ventures?
20. How could it become the foundation of a diversified collection of profitable businesses?

"If you cannot answer a question confidently, identify the specific evidence needed to resolve it."
