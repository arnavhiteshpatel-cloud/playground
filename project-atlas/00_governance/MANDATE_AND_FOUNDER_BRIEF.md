# Project Atlas — Mandate and Founder Brief (read this first)

This is the condensed mandate every Project Atlas researcher works from. The full founder prompt is
preserved in `00_governance/FOUNDER_PROMPT_SUMMARY.md` (section map) — this brief is authoritative for agents.

## The question
**What is the strongest business the founder can establish from scratch today that becomes a substantial,
profitable, cash-generating operating company — the first asset of a future diversified holding company?**

Success = sustainable free cash flow and owner wealth, not revenue, not valuation, not technical novelty.
A conventional business producing $500k/yr sustainable FCF beats a speculative startup.

## Hard boundaries of the search
- **Industry agnostic.** Every legitimate industry competes on equal terms. Examples in the founder's prompt
  (loan brokerage, procurement, middlemen, distribution) are illustrations, NOT preferences. Do not over-weight them.
- **Start from scratch.** Acquiring an existing business is NOT an allowed initial entry (acquisitions are a later
  capital-allocation tool). Franchises are allowed as candidates but must be judged on owner economics after fees.
- **Not venture-style.** No requirement for patents, proprietary AI, novel tech, VC interest, billion-dollar TAM.
  A software startup is not excluded, but it gets no bonus for being software and is judged on cash economics.
- **Not passive initially.** The founder will sell, negotiate, do customer service, work long hours, travel, hire.
  But the business must eventually not be capped by the founder's personal hours (delegation matters).
- Separate from the founder's tech ventures (OsteoQuant, Concord). Do not reuse them.

## Founder resources and circumstances
- Up to ~$100,000 **conditional** personal backing. NOT a spending target. Strong preference: validate for
  hundreds of dollars, launch for low thousands, deploy more only after demand is proven. Capital-intensive ideas
  are allowed only if economics justify them.
- Location: **Illinois** (Chicago-area access assumed plausible; confirm nothing that depends on a specific city).
  Broader U.S. market reachable.
- Background: technology, research, entrepreneurship. **No known professional licenses** (not a CPA, attorney,
  physician, PE, licensed contractor, insurance producer, MLO, real-estate broker). Age and credit history unknown —
  flag any opportunity whose feasibility depends on them (e.g., age-18/21 minimums, personal credit for bonds).
- Willing to learn unfamiliar industries, do direct sales, build supplier relationships, hire and train.
- Has heavy Claude/Claude Code access **only until 5 December 2026** (today is 28 November 2026). After that, AI
  usage must be affordable/ordinary. The business must not depend on unlimited AI.

## Financial milestones to assess (analytical, not forecasts)
First profitable transaction → $1k/mo → $5k/mo → $10k/mo → $25k/mo operating profit → $100k/yr → $250k/yr →
$500k/yr → $1M/yr+. For each opportunity, judge which milestones are *plausible for a capable new entrant*.
Never manufacture a path to $1M.

## What counts
Operating profit, free cash flow, distributable cash, ROIC, incremental return on growth capital, cash
conversion, working-capital needs, resilience, delegation, customer/supplier diversification, long-term business
value, risk-adjusted returns, founder-time opportunity cost.
Distinguish revenue ≠ profit ≠ cash; accounting profit ≠ distributable cash.

## Authorization boundaries (strict)
Allowed: lawful research of public information; analysis; local software, databases, models; drafts.
**Not allowed:** spending money, contracting, creating accounts, registering anything, submitting bids or forms,
applying for anything, contacting any customer/supplier/person, sending any communication, representing the
founder. Respect robots/ToS and access controls; never bypass paywalls or logins; never fabricate data.

## Evidence discipline (summary — see EVIDENCE_STANDARDS.md)
Every consequential number carries an evidence level A–E and a source ID. Missing information is recorded as
missing (null / "MISSING"), never invented. Mature-incumbent economics ≠ new-entrant economics.
