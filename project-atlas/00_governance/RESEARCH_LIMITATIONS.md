# Research Limitations (living document)

1. **No external contact authorized.** Supplier accessibility, partner qualification, pricing quotes, and customer
   interest cannot be confirmed — only documented from public materials or marked as assumptions. Every finalist
   therefore carries explicit "required confirmations".
2. **Private-company economics.** Small private operators rarely publish financials. We rely on FDD Item 19
   (franchisor-disclosed), public-company comps (incumbent economics), Census aggregates, trade surveys, and
   business-for-sale listings (D-level, seller-claimed). New-entrant economics are modeled (E) from these.
3. **SEC EDGAR programmatic access** requires a declared contact in the User-Agent; we do not send the founder's
   personal details to third parties, so bulk EDGAR pulls were not used. Filings were read through web search/fetch.
4. **Paywalled market research** (IBISWorld, Frost, Gartner, RMA Annual Statement Studies full data) is not
   accessible; only publicly available summaries are used and labeled.
5. **Time sensitivity.** Rules, programs, commission schedules, tariffs and prices change; every source carries an
   access date (2026-11-28 onward). Re-verify before acting.
6. **Founder facts unknown:** age, credit history, veteran/minority/women-owned status (set-aside eligibility),
   exact location within Illinois, available hours, network. Opportunities depending on these are flagged.
7. **Desk research cannot measure sales skill or local relationship advantages**; customer-acquisition estimates are
   ranges with stated evidence levels and must be tested with the validation experiments.
8. **Shared web-search budget.** The WebSearch tool's budget is shared across parallel agents and was exhausted
   during Stage A (WS09 reported exhaustion after ~10 searches). Agents switched to direct fetches of known
   primary-source pages (agency sites, filings, program pages). Later stages are instructed to prefer direct fetches
   of known URLs and reserve search for discovery of unknown sources. Some Illinois sites (ilga.gov statutes, IBHE)
   returned HTTP 503 during the research window; affected statute details are marked unverified.
