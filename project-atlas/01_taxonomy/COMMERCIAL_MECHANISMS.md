## Observations (Stage B)
- Mechanisms built on **mandated, recurring, route-dense service** (inspection_testing, recurring_service_contract, geographic_density, compliance_workflow, route_business) and **vertical managed services** cluster at the top.
- **Buy-resell, marketplace, private-label consumer and information products** score lowest on average: platform dependence, CAC, inventory, tariff exposure, and (for information products) AI commoditization.
- **Brokerage/commission** splits: insurance renewals and fee-based managed services hold up; transactional brokerage without recurrence (loan brokerage, freight agent) weakened after blind re-scoring.
- **Franchise** units rank mid-pack at best after royalties and capital; none advanced to Stage C.
