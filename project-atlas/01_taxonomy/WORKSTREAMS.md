# Discovery Workstreams (Stage A)

Each workstream records ~25–35 meaningfully distinct opportunities (more if genuinely distinct, fewer if the
space is thin — explain). Coverage hints are **starting points, not a checklist and not a preference**; actively
look for opportunities not listed. Stay within your boundary; put outside ideas under "Cross-stream leads".
