# WS08 — Government & Institutional Procurement: Discovery Summary

33 records, 43 sources (accessed 2026-11-28). Composite scores are deliberately harsh (max evidence-adjusted ~5.6). Public procurement has A-level *demand* data (award records). It has almost no A/B evidence of what small resellers *keep*.

## Verified rules (primary sources)
- **Thresholds unchanged since 2025-10-01.** The micro-purchase threshold (MPT) is **$15,000** and the simplified acquisition threshold (SAT) is **$350,000**. Both are still in FAR 2.101 under FAC 2026-01 (effective 2026-03-13) (S001/S002).
  - MPT exceptions: $2,000 for Davis-Bacon construction, $2,500 for Service Contract Act services, $25k/$40k for emergencies.
  - The FY2026 NDAA (signed 2025-12-18) raised DoD cost-data (TINA), Cost Accounting Standards and sole-source approval thresholds. I found no change to the MPT or SAT; the House $25k/$500k proposals were apparently not enacted (S006/S007).
  - NDAA Sec. 824 requires DoD guidance by December 2026 on accepting non-government past performance.
- **Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO):** being implemented through class deviations; formal rulemaking was pending in 2026.
  - Buys above the MPT and up to the SAT must still be set aside for small business (S003/S004).
  - The RFO, EO 14240 (consolidating common-goods buying into GSA) and NDAA Sec. 812 push buying toward GSA schedules and consolidated contracts, and make set-asides on orders under those contracts discretionary (S005/S032). **This is a long-run headwind for open-market resellers.**
- **Nonmanufacturer rule:** a reseller counts as small at ≤500 employees. The rule does *not* apply to set-asides between the MPT and SAT, so resale of large-manufacturer brands is allowed below $350k (S012).
- **Payment:**
  - Federal: due 30 days after a proper invoice or acceptance; construction progress payments in 14 days; perishables in 10. Late interest is 4.75% (S009/S010).
  - Illinois: general-funds accounts payable were **$1.45B (17,398 vouchers)** on 2026-11-28 (S028).
- **Bonding:** Miller Act bonds above $150k; alternative payment protections from $35k to $150k (S011). Bonding capacity depends on the founder's credit, which is unknown.
- **Set-asides, FY2025:** small business $34.7B, 8(a) $14.1B, SDVOSB $8.7B, WOSB $1.4B, HUBZone $0.92B (S036).
  - VA purchase orders: $505M set aside for SDVOSBs vs $138M for general small business (S024). All set-asides were treated as conditional.
- **Registration and schedules:** SAM.gov must be renewed yearly (S031). A GSA schedule needs 2 years of corporate experience unless the firm uses Startup Springboard (S029).

## Strongest candidates
1. **WS08-001 DLA DIBBS dealer (advance).**
   - DLA spent $58.3B in FY2025, $24.6B with small businesses (S017). About 6,400 small vendors had revenue: the ~2,000th earned ~$0.5M and the ~3,000th ~$0.18M (S018).
   - 206 Illinois small firms won DLA work (S019).
   - Auto-evaluated RFQs at or below the MPT are not set aside (S033).
   - This is the most open door: buyers post daily, awards are price-based, and no relationships are needed. Unknowns: margins and supplier access.
2. **WS08-017 E-Rate Category 2 integrator (advance; best founder fit).**
   - Illinois FY2025: $42M across 150 providers. CDW has 32%; the 50th provider had $118k; FRNs averaged about 3 bids.
   - Nationally: $1.52B across 1,907 providers, of which 522 exceed $250k and 230 exceed $1M. Funded demand has been stable at $1.3–1.6B a year (S025/S041/S042).
   - Buyers post bid requests publicly (Form 470). Risks: USAC payment lag and Universal Service Fund policy.
3. **WS08-003 Federal open-market set-aside reseller (advance; run together with 001).** FY2025 product purchase orders: $11.3B in total, $7.5B to small businesses, $3.1B formally set aside (S020).
4. **WS08-005 Bureau of Prisons food bids (hold).** $155M in small-business product POs, about $131M of it food, averaging ~$27k per PO (S023). Demand is mandated, but distributor margins are thin (S037).
5. **WS08-024 Turnout-gear care route (hold, evidence E).** Recurring, standards-driven demand from public buyers. Unverified.
6. **WS08-012 HUBZone overlay (hold).** Status is achievable through office location and hiring, not demographics (S035). The national pool is small.
7. **WS08-015 Public-works supply dealer and WS08-002 DLA alternate-offer manufacturing (hold).** Plausible extensions; evidence E.

## Surprises
- **DLA's "small business" leaders are huge:** Atlantic Diving Supply $5.6B, W.S. Darley $1.47B (S018/S019).
- **Illinois federal custodial and grounds work is held by nonprofit, AbilityOne-type mandatory sources** (S016/S022). Incumbent operating margins are 3–4% (S037).
- **Chicago approved only 8 new commodity contracts in 2025** (ceilings totalling $107.7M) and 4 small-order contracts (S027).
- **Card micro-purchases do not appear in award data** (S008).

## Looks good but fails
- Generic school, IT or office resale (016, 030): CDW runs at 21.7% gross and 7.4% operating margin (S037).
- VA selling without veteran status (011).
- Federal janitorial (009).
- Chicago/Cook County as a primary channel (014).
- Hospital GPOs (025).
- Disaster contracting (029).
- DoD Fresh produce (032): fails the capital gate.
- Federal consulting (007): capped by founder hours.
- A GSA schedule as the entry point (006). A schedule is not demand.

## Margins, cash and barriers
- **Margins:** reseller gross margins of 8–30% are analyst estimates (E). Incumbents show 18–41% gross and 3–14% operating margins (S037).
- **Cash:** federal payment is roughly net-30 after acceptance; Illinois state and local payment is slower.
- **Past performance:** the main wall for services and construction. Price-based supply resale under the SAT largely avoids it.

## Evidence gaps
1. Dealer margins and win rates for DIBBS and simplified-acquisition resale.
2. Whether new resellers can get supplier pricing on approved-source parts.
3. USAC payment timing, and Illinois licensing and prevailing-wage rules for school network installs.
4. Illinois statutes, unverified because ILGA returned 503: small purchase and school bid thresholds, prompt-payment and bond-act terms, CMS small-business set-aside rules.
5. Not retrieved: BidBuy, Cook County and university award data; cooperative-contract fees and sales; GSA Schedule sales and IFF rate; Chicago HUBZone map coverage.

## Limitations

- Blocked sites: dla.mil returned 403, so DIBBS facts come from search extracts only; eCFR redirected to a block page, so CFR text comes from the Cornell LII mirror.
- USASpending counts are awards with any FY2025 action. Totals for awards ≤$350k include delivery orders under large contracts.
