# WS08 — Government & Institutional Procurement: Discovery Summary

33 records, 43 sources (access date 2026-11-28). Composite scores are deliberately low (max evidence-adjusted ~5.6). In public procurement, demand is unusually well documented: award data is A-level. Margins are not. No A/B source was found for what small resellers actually keep after costs.

## Rules and thresholds verified (primary sources)
- **The thresholds have not changed since October 2025.** The micro-purchase threshold (MPT) is **$15,000** and the simplified acquisition threshold (SAT) is **$350,000**. These come from the FAR inflation rule effective 2025-10-01 and are still in FAR 2.101 under FAC 2026-01 (effective 2026-03-13) (WS08-S001/S002).
  - Exceptions to the $15k MPT: $2,000 for Davis-Bacon construction, $2,500 for Service Contract Act services, and $25k/$40k for emergencies.
  - The FY2026 NDAA (signed 2025-12-18) raised DoD's truthful-cost-data (TINA) threshold, its Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) thresholds and its sole-source approval levels. I found no evidence that it changed the MPT or SAT. The House proposals ($25k MPT / $500k SAT, H.R. 4123) appear not to have been enacted (S006/S007).
  - NDAA Sec. 824 directs DoD guidance by December 2026 on accepting *non-government* past performance. That is a modest tailwind for new entrants.
- **Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO)** is still being implemented through agency class deviations; formal rulemaking was pending in 2026 (S004/S005).
  - Purchases above the MPT and at or below the SAT must still be set aside for small business (FAR 13.003; RFO Part 13).
  - But the RFO, EO 14240 (consolidating common-goods buying into GSA) and NDAA Sec. 812 all push buying toward GSA schedules and other consolidated vehicles, and make set-asides on orders under multiple-award contracts discretionary (S005/S032). **Net effect: a long-run headwind for open-market small resellers.**
- **Nonmanufacturer rule (13 CFR 121.406).** Resellers qualify as small at ≤500 employees. The rule *does not apply* to small-business set-asides between the MPT and SAT, so below $350k a small reseller can supply large-manufacturer products (S012).
- **Payment.**
  - Federal invoices are due 30 days after a proper invoice or acceptance (7-day constructive acceptance). Construction progress payments are due in 14 days, perishables in 10, meat in 7. Late-payment interest is 4.75% for July–December 2026 (S009/S010).
  - Illinois general-funds accounts payable were **$1.45B (17,398 vouchers) on 2026-11-28** (S028). State payment risk is real.
- **Bonding.** Miller Act bonds are required above **$150k**, with alternative payment protections from $35k to $150k (S011). Bonding capacity depends on personal credit, and the founder's credit is unknown.
- **Set-asides by type, FY2025:** small business $34.7B, 8(a) $14.1B, SDVOSB $8.7B, WOSB $1.4B, HUBZone $0.92B (S036).
  - VA purchase orders are dominated by Veterans First: SDVOSB set-asides were **$505M vs $138M** for general small-business set-asides (S015/S024). All set-asides were treated as conditional.
- **Other rules.** SAM.gov registration must be renewed every 365 days (S031). A GSA schedule normally requires 2 years of corporate experience; Startup Springboard is the exception path (S029).

## Strongest candidates
1. **WS08-001 DLA DIBBS spare-parts dealer (advance).** This is the most open door in public procurement.
   - DLA spent $58.3B in FY2025, $24.6B of it with small businesses (S017).
   - About 6,400 small businesses had DLA revenue. The ~2,000th earned about $0.5M and the ~3,000th about $0.18M (S018). That gives an A-level ladder of realistic revenue, and it is mostly modest.
   - 206 Illinois small firms won DLA work (S019).
   - Fast Auto Evaluation RFQs at or below the MPT are not set aside and are awarded automatically (S033).
   - Unknowns: dealer margins and access to suppliers for approved-source parts.
2. **WS08-017 E-Rate Category 2 network integrator (advance; best founder fit).** All figures are from USAC open data (S025/S041/S042).
   - Illinois FY2025: $42M across 150 providers. CDW has 32%; the 50th provider had $118k. FRNs averaged about 3 bids each.
   - Nationally: $1.52B across 1,907 providers, of which 522 exceed $250k and 230 exceed $1M.
   - Funded internal-connections demand has been stable at $1.3–1.6B a year, and FY2026 requests are $4.06B.
   - Buyers post their needs publicly (FCC Form 470). Open risks: USAC payment lag and Universal Service Fund policy.
3. **WS08-003 Federal open-market set-aside reseller (advance; run together with 001).**
   - FY2025 product purchase orders: $11.3B in total, $7.5B to small businesses, $3.1B explicitly set aside (S020).
   - Policy consolidation is the main threat.
4. **WS08-005 Bureau of Prisons food commodity supplier (hold).**
   - BOP's small-business product POs totalled $155M, about $131M of it food; the average PO is about $27k (S023).
   - Mandated, recurring demand awarded on public, price-based bids. Distributor margins are thin (Sysco's operating margin is 3.7% as an incumbent; S037).
5. **WS08-024 Fire turnout-gear cleaning & inspection route (hold, evidence E).** Recurring, standards-driven demand from public buyers. Nothing about it is verified yet.
6. **WS08-012 HUBZone overlay (hold).** HUBZone status depends on where the office is and who is hired, not on the founder's demographics (S035). The national pool is small ($0.92B).
7. **WS08-015 Local public-works supply dealer and WS08-002 DLA alternate-offer manufacturing (hold).** Plausible extensions; the evidence is E.

## Surprises
- **"Small business" DLA rankings are led by giants.** Atlantic Diving Supply received $5.6B, W.S. Darley $1.47B and SupplyCore $779M (S018/S019). Price competition is extreme, and automated bidders set the price.
- **Federal custodial and grounds work in Illinois is largely held by nonprofit, AbilityOne-type providers.** They are mandatory sources under 41 USC 8504 (S016/S022). Incumbent operating margins are 3–4% (ABM, BrightView; S037).
- **City of Chicago contracting is closed to newcomers.** It approved only 8 new commodity contracts in 2025, with combined ceilings of $107.7M, and 4 small-order contracts (S027).
- **Card micro-purchases are invisible in award data** (FAR 4.606; S008). Local purchase-card demand cannot be measured from public data.

## Looks good but fails
- Generic school, IT or office resale (WS08-016, WS08-030): CDW runs at 21.7% gross margin and 7.4% operating margin with scale purchasing.
- VA selling without veteran status (WS08-011).
- Federal janitorial (WS08-009).
- Chicago/Cook County as a primary channel (WS08-014).
- Hospital GPOs (WS08-025).
- Disaster contracting (WS08-029).
- USDA DoD Fresh produce (WS08-032): capital gate failure, likely more than $100k before first revenue.
- Federal research/IT consulting (WS08-007): capped by founder hours.
- A GSA schedule as an *entry* point (WS08-006). A schedule is a licence to compete, not demand.

## Margins, working capital and barriers (honest view)
- **Margins.** Reseller gross margins of 8–30% are analyst estimates (E). Incumbent 10-Ks show 18–41% gross and 3–14% operating margins (S037). A new entrant will earn less.
- **Working capital.** The federal cash cycle is roughly net-30 after acceptance, against suppliers who are prepaid or on net-30. Illinois state and local payment is slower, and the backlog is documented.
- **Barriers.**
  - Past performance is the main wall for services and construction.
  - Supply resale under the SAT avoids most of it, because awards are price-based and DIBBS is automated.
  - Bonding matters only for construction above $35k–$150k.

## Limitations
- The shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through. The rest of the research used direct fetches of known primary URLs and public APIs (USASpending, USAC, Chicago data portal, SEC XBRL).
- Some sites could not be fetched directly:
  - dla.mil returned 403, so DIBBS facts come from search extracts only;
  - ilga.gov returned 503;
  - eCFR redirected to a block page, so CFR text comes from the Cornell LII mirror.
- USASpending counts are awards with any FY2025 action. Totals for awards ≤$350k include delivery orders under large contracts.
