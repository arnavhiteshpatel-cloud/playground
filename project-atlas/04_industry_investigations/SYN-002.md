# SYN-002 — Independent dental & veterinary practice operations bundle · Stage C

The bundle has five parts: managed IT (WS04-014), insurance verification/AR (WS04-011), OSHA/HIPAA compliance (WS04-021), spore testing (WS04-026) and medical-waste pickup (WS04-024).

> **Verdict: DROP the five-part bundle as defined.** Carry forward a two-part core, managed IT plus HIPAA/OSHA compliance, inside WS04-014 at Stage D.
>
> **Revised evidence level:** D. Component prices are now documented at level B/D. Attach rates are E.
>
> **Why the full bundle fails:**
> - **Bundling is real but happens in pairs.** WM sells compliance with waste pickup, HealthFirst sells spore tests with a compliance portal, and distributors sell technology with supplies. We found no seller offering the five-way package.
> - **Little shared cost.** The five parts use five different operating competencies: IT technicians, billers, trainers, a permitted hazmat route and lab logistics. They share only the sales motion.
> - **Two components lose money at small scale.** Spore testing is a $228–490 per sterilizer per year commodity. Waste pickup brings $40–180 a month per office, and a self-performed route costs more than that until it reaches about 150 customers.
> - **The full bundle barely beats MSP alone.** At realistic attach rates, the self-performed bundle adds about $4k to year-3 profit and needs $40–90k more capital. A two-part MSP + compliance bundle adds about $18k (+15%), and adding verification brings the gain to about $35k (+28%).
> - **Consolidation is not the binding risk.** Illinois DSO affiliation rose only from 12.7% to 13.9% over 2022–25.

## 1. Industry structure and consolidation test
- **Practice counts.** Illinois had 5,781 dental and 1,159 veterinary establishments in 2023 [C, C6-S003]. The 2019 counts were 5,940 and 1,100 [C, C6-S004].
- **Dental consolidation is slow in Illinois:**
  - DSO-affiliated dentists rose from 12.7% (2022) to 13.9% (2025), about +0.4 pp a year. Nationally the share went from 12.7% to 17.0% [C, C6-S001, C6-S002].
  - Dentists in single-location practices fell from 75.0% to 71.2% over the same period.
  - Young dentists are the leading indicator: 25.2% of those 0–5 years out of school are DSO-affiliated in Illinois [C, C6-S001].
  - Implied erosion of the independent base is roughly 1% a year (E).
- **Veterinary consolidation:**
  - Corporate groups own about 25% of primary-care practices and about 75% of specialty/ER practices, and the pace is slowing [C, C6-S005].
  - Illinois veterinary establishments rose 5.4% from 2019 to 2023 [C].
  - The Illinois corporate share of veterinary practices is MISSING.

## 2. Value chain & mechanism
The same office manager is the buyer, but the purchases differ:

| Component | Decision-maker | Purchase type |
|---|---|---|
| IT | Owner signs | Relationship purchase |
| Verification/billing | Owner and manager | Cash-sensitive |
| Compliance | Office manager | Annual subscription |
| Spore tests | Office manager | Consumable bought with supplies |
| Waste | Office manager | Contract with auto-renewal |

Margin sits in IT (labor leverage) and compliance (content leverage). Waste margin sits with whoever owns treatment.

## 4. Small-start evidence
Each component has small vendors: Siotek, Darkhorse, eAssist, small medical-waste haulers and university spore labs. We found no small firm selling all five (MISSING; not exhaustively searched).

## 5. Competition
- **Distributors (Henry Schein, Patterson, Benco).** They already bundle technology, compliance products and supplies through reps who visit monthly [B, WS04-S013, WS04-S026].
- **WM.** It bundles waste pickup with compliance training [B, C6-S012].
- **HealthFirst.** It bundles spore tests, a compliance dashboard and emergency kits [B, C6-S013].
- **Where the gap is.** It is narrow: local, on-site IT depth combined with done-for-you HIPAA risk analysis.

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- Contract cycles differ by component:
  - Waste: 3–5-year auto-renewing contracts [D, C6-S011].
  - Spore tests: autoship consumables.
  - IT: annual or month-to-month.
- Consolidating vendors therefore means waiting for each contract to expire. There is no evidence that independents prefer a single vendor (MISSING; would need interviews).
- The office manager's real pain points are IT outages and insurance claims. Spore testing and waste are low-attention purchases.

## 7. Failure evidence
- **Operational sprawl.** Five compliance regimes apply:
  - HIPAA business-associate obligations
  - IEPA PIMW permit and manifests [A, WS04-S016]
  - DOT hazmat
  - OSHA trainer competence
  - Lab quality control
- **Thin and lossy components.** Waste pickup has thin tickets and competitor-owned treatment [A, C6-S010]. Spore testing is a low-ticket commodity.
- **Consolidation churn.** About 1% of the base a year is lost, which is a real but slow leak.

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **The wedge:** "What a DSO gives its offices, for independents": one local account manager, one monthly invoice, and a compliance binder.
- **Is it real?** For IT plus HIPAA/OSHA compliance plus optional verification, yes. These lines share data and access, and the risk analysis is IT work.
- **What to do with waste and spore testing:** they are better as referral partnerships, earning a referral fee or nothing, than as self-performed lines.
- **Can it be copied?** Distributors can copy it, but their reps are product-focused.

## 10. Economics sanity check — year 3 (does the bundle beat its best component?)
The best single component is WS04-014 MSP, with a base case of 35 offices and about **$120k** operating profit (see WS04-014 §10).

Increments from add-ons, using E-level attach rates:

| Add-on | Calculation | Year-3 increment |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance | 50% × 35 × $125/mo × 12 × 70% margin | +$18.4k |
| Verification | 20% × 35 = 7 offices × $600 × 12 × 30% margin | +$15.1k |
| Spore testing | 30% × 35 × 1.5 sterilizers ≈ 16 × $300 × 35% | +$1.7k |
| Waste, self-performed | 9 accounts × $85 × 12 = $9.2k revenue vs ≥$40k route fixed cost (van, driver share, permit, insurance) | about −$31k |
| Waste, referred to a hauler | Referral margin about 10% | +$0.9k |

Totals:
- **Full self-performed bundle ≈ $124k**, essentially the same as MSP alone, with $40–90k more capital at risk.
- **Trimmed bundle (MSP + compliance + verification, waste/spore referred) ≈ $156k (+30%).**

Range for the defined bundle:
- **Conservative ≈ −$10k:** the MSP conservative case of $13k, plus small add-ons, minus losses on the waste route.
- **Optimistic ≈ $300k.**

Capital for the defined bundle: $55–140k.
