# WS02-001: Trucking / motor-carrier niche commercial insurance agency (Stage C, batch C4)

> **Verdict: HOLD as a standalone business.** Merge it into SYN-001 as that bundle's profit engine.
> Evidence: **C** (unchanged). The commission pool is official statistics; the realized split for a start-up is D.
>
> **Why.**
> - **The commission pool is real but bounded.** Illinois commercial-auto "selling expense" (commission, brokerage and
>   other acquisition costs) is 15.9% of premium [C, C4-S015]. A start-up placing business through
>   wholesalers/MGAs/networks keeps roughly 8–11% of premium (D/E).
> - **The line loses money for insurers.** Commercial auto liability ran a −15.1% underwriting result countrywide in
>   2024; Illinois commercial auto overall ran −4.7% [C, C4-S015]. That means scarce, volatile capacity for trucking.
> - **The lead pool churns hard.** 44.7% of Chicago single-truck carriers registered in 2024 or later [A, C4-S002].
>   Of the 2023 for-hire cohort, only 41.9% are still active [A, C4-S001].
> - **Result:** a founder-produced agency reaches about $25–130k of year-3 operating profit, below Stage A's $60–250k.
>   Pairing it with a compliance subscription (SYN-001) improves lead flow and retention.

## Industry structure
- **Who must buy.** Every for-hire carrier needs liability insurance filed with FMCSA. In the 8 counties, 18,502 active
  carriers with 1–100 power units (PU) hold active MC authority [A, C4-S002].
- **Premium per truck (C).** ATRI puts average premium at 10.6¢/mile in 2025. Small fleets pay nearly double the
  per-mile rate of large fleets [C, WS02-S017]. At about 100k miles, that is roughly $10–20k per truck per year (E).
- **Market size.** National commercial auto net premiums earned were about $60.4B in 2024, all segments [C, C4-S015].
  Illinois commercial auto was about $2.78B (direct-basis state table) [C, C4-S015].
- **Agency count.** About 37,000 independent agencies [C, WS02-S018]. The number of trucking specialists is MISSING.
- **New-venture flow.** For-hire registrations in the Chicago area were flat in 2026 (−3% vs 2025, same period)
  [A, C4-S001], despite FMCSA's new vetting [C, WS02-S030].

## Value chain & mechanism
1. The carrier pays premium to the insurer or MGA, often financed.
2. The commission flows down: insurer → MGA/wholesaler → retail agent.
3. Return commissions apply when policies cancel for non-payment, which is common in trucking (E).
4. The agency also does a lot of unpaid servicing: BMC-91X/MCS-90 filings, certificates of insurance (COIs) for every
   new broker, driver additions, claims.
5. Margin is captured in the renewal book. Agencies above $1M EBITDA trade at about 11.4x; sub-$1M books trade far
   lower [C, WS02-S007].

## Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** the owner.
- **When:** at authority activation (must file before operating) and at each renewal, shopping 30–60 days out (E).
- **Spend:** about $14k × trucks per year (E/C).
- **Payment:** premium finance is common.
- **How they find agents:** FMCSA-lead telemarketers, referrals from dispatchers and factoring firms, community
  networks, Google.

## Failure evidence
- **Book erosion from carrier mortality** [A, C4-S001]: 2021 for-hire cohort 29.5% surviving; 2022 30.7%.
- **Capacity withdrawals** after underwriting losses [C, C4-S015].
- **Chargebacks** on non-pay cancellations (E).
- **E&O** from missed filings (Stage A).
- Enforcement cases: none collected (MISSING).

## Regulation
- **Illinois resident P&C producer licence:** 20 hours of pre-licensing per line; General and State exams; $215; age
  18+ [A, WS02-S001, S003].
- **Agency entity licence:** $150, with a designated responsible producer [A, WS02-S003].
- **Surplus lines:** avoided by placing E&S business through licensed wholesalers (3.5% tax plus stamping fee are
  theirs) [A/C, WS02-S004–S005].
- **Time:** about 1–3 months.
- **E&O:** expected by carriers and networks; cost MISSING.
- **Licensed employees:** CSRs need licences too.

## Entry wedge test
- **Why switch:**
  - same-day filings and COIs;
  - a safety and claims coaching plan that improves the next renewal;
  - language-community trust.
- **How it is delivered:** compliance data from SYN-001 is used to market risk improvements to underwriters.
- **Copyable:** yes, and Simplex and OOIDA already pair the two.
- **Standalone wedge:** weak.
