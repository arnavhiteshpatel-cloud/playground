# WS02-018 — Insurance-agency back-office outsourcing (COIs, endorsements, renewals, AMS data) (Stage C, batch C5)

Access date 2026-11-28.

| Verdict | **DROP** |
|---|---|
| Revised evidence level | **C**. Published vendor prices are B, market structure is C, and new-entrant margin is E. |
| Reason | This is a commodity labor-arbitrage market with transparent prices well below the Stage A assumption. One vendor publishes Philippine insurance VAs handling COIs, endorsements and AMS work at **$1,040–$1,600 per full-time month**, with no setup fee and 1,000+ VAs placed [B, C5-S026]. Stage A assumed $1,500–$3,000. The buyer pool is shrinking: about 39,000 agencies in 2024 became about 37,000 in 2026 [C, WS02-S018]. The work being outsourced (COI issuance, document review) is exactly what AI is absorbing. Agency AI adoption went from 15% to 46% in two years [C, WS02-S018]; adjacent COI software already reviews a certificate in about 30 seconds [B, C5-S027]; and digital insurers issue COIs instantly [B, C5-S025]. Base year-3 operating profit is about $50k on a declining product. |

## 1. Industry structure
- **Buyers:**
  - About 37,000 US independent agencies, averaging 9.9 staff [C, WS02-S018].
  - Illinois has 5,424 agency firms, but 4,093 of them have fewer than 5 employees, averaging about $302k receipts [C, C5-S001]. Most are too small to buy a full FTE.
- **Seller industry:** offshore BPO and VA staffing (Philippines, India, LatAm). These firms are numerous and visible in search results (vendor guides, D).
- **Growth:** buyer count is falling. Outsourcing demand per agency rises with labor costs, but AI substitutes for it.

## 2. Value chain and mechanism
- An agency pays a monthly fee per full-time-equivalent (FTE) or per task.
- The BPO recruits, trains and supervises offshore staff working in the agency's management system (AMS: Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft, EZLynx, NowCerts).
- **Where margin is captured:** the spread between offshore wages and the per-FTE bill rate, minus supervision, QA and attrition costs.
- **Licensed work stays with the agency:** coverage advice and binding must be done by licensed agents. One vendor explicitly excludes them [B, C5-S026].

## 3. Operator economics
| Operator / benchmark | Numbers | Level |
|---|---|---|
| VA MASTERS | $6.50–$10/hr; $1,040–$1,600/month full-time; $520–$800 part-time; no setup fee; 500+ clients, 1,000+ VAs placed | B (price) / D (counts), C5-S026 |
| Marketplace data | Insurance-experienced Philippine talent $5–7/hr (median $6, about $960/month) | D, C5-S037 |
| US benchmark (vendor-cited) | US insurance CSR $35–50k/yr | D |
| Agency margins | Best-practices agencies 23–31% EBITDA, with labor the main cost lever | C, WS02-S008 |

**New entrant vs incumbents:**
- To compete, a new entrant must price at or below about $1,400/FTE-month.
- With staff cost of about $750 plus about $100 in benefits and equipment, gross margin is about **$550 per FTE-month (≈39%)** [E].
- Incumbent BPOs have recruiting pipelines and brand; the entrant has neither.

## 4. Small-start evidence
- Vendor claims of 500+ clients suggest small firms can grow in this niche (D).
- No financials were found: MISSING.

## 5. Competition
- Many offshore VA firms with published prices (D/B).
- Larger insurance BPOs (Patra and similar; not verified this session).
- AMS vendors adding automation (not verified).
- AI tools that agencies adopt directly; adoption moved from 15% to 46% [C].
- Carriers' own service centers (not verified).

**Weaknesses:**
- Turnover.
- QA errors that create E&O exposure for the agency.

**Switching costs:** low. The vendor offers free replacement and no setup fee [B, C5-S026], so customers can leave easily too.

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** the agency owner or operations manager.
- **What they buy:** a dedicated VA or a pooled team.
- **Discovery:** vendor search, association vendor lists, peer referrals.
- **Contracts:** month-to-month with a service agreement (B).
- **Selection criteria:** price per hour, AMS familiarity and replacement guarantees. That is a buying process that commoditizes the vendor.

## 7. Failure evidence
- **AI substitution of the core tasks:**
  - COI extraction in 30 seconds versus 30 minutes manually (vendor claim) [B, C5-S027].
  - Instant COIs from digital carriers [B, C5-S025].
- **Shrinking agency count** through M&A: 695 deals in 2025 [C, WS02-S006]. Acquirers centralize back offices in-house or with scale BPOs.
- **E&O risk:** quality errors (wrong certificate holder, missed endorsement) are a generic risk; no specific enforcement found.

## 8. Regulation
- No licence is required for clerical tasks.
- Licensed tasks need licensed staff. An Illinois non-resident producer licence costs $380 [A, WS02-S003].
- **GLBA / state privacy:** client NPI handled offshore needs safeguards and contract terms (not verified for Illinois specifics) [E].
- **Offshore staffing:** employment via an employer-of-record or contractor structure abroad. Foreign labor law is not researched (MISSING).
- **Gate:** G4_CEILING WARN.

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **Proposed wedge:** specialize in one AMS and one niche (e.g., trucking COIs) with SLAs and AI QA.
- **Why it fails:**
  - Vendors already advertise AMS-specific staff across all major systems [B, C5-S026].
  - AI QA is available to the agency directly.
  - The wedge is copyable within weeks and shrinks the FTE count the business bills on.

## 10. Economics sanity check (year 3) [E]
**Low:** 8 FTE × $500 × 12 = $48k gross margin.
- Costs: about $40k for part-time supervision, recruiting and tools.
- **Operating profit about $8k.**

**Base:** 25 FTE × $550 × 12 = $165k gross margin.
- Costs: US account manager/QA $65k; recruiting/training $15k; security/software/insurance $15k; sales $20k.
- **Operating profit about $50k.**

**High:** 60 FTE × $600 × 12 = $432k gross margin.
- Costs: about $200k for managers, PH team leads, recruiting and overhead.
- **Operating profit about $232k.**
- This requires winning about 30–60 agencies against established vendors while AI reduces seats per agency.

**Capital:** about $5–30k, including staff payroll that precedes collection.
