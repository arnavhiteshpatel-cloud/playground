## 1. Industry structure
- **US, NAICS 541214 Payroll Services** (SUSB 2022) [C, C5-S001]:
  - 4,484 firms with $27.3B receipts.
  - 194 firms with 500+ employees take $17.3B (63%).
  - The 3,252 firms with fewer than 20 employees take $2.6B, averaging $799k.
- **Illinois:**
  - 154 firms, with $1.76B receipts dominated by 17 large enterprises.
  - Small bureaus are scarce and small: 91 firms with fewer than 20 employees ($51.5M total), and 58 with fewer than 5 (about $253k average).
- **Demand base:** essential and recurring. Every employer runs payroll.
- **Cyclicality:** low to moderate. Client counts fall with business failures; Paychex retains only 82–83% of payroll clients a year [A, C5-S011].

## 2. Value chain and mechanism
- The employer pays a per-run or monthly fee.
- The platform (ADP/Paychex/Intuit/Gusto, or a white-label engine) calculates pay, impounds and remits taxes, and files 941/940/state returns.
- A bureau adds:
  - data collection (timecards by job/class);
  - exception handling;
  - notices;
  - certified payroll.
- Margin is captured by whoever holds the platform and the float. Paychex earns about $6.1k/client/year in Management Solutions revenue at a 38.6% operating margin (incumbent economics, including HR modules) [A, C5-S011, WS02-S022].
- Check, the leading embedded-payroll API, markets to software platforms, not bureaus [C, C5-S012].
- White-label bureau platform terms (iSolved, Asure/Evolution, accountant-channel programs) were **MISSING** this session.

## 3. Operator economics
| Operator | Numbers | Level |

| QuickBooks Payroll | $50 + $7/employee; $88 + $13; $134 + $17 (Elite adds tax-penalty protection up to $25k/yr). | B, C5-S010 |

| Illinois micro bureaus | 58 firms averaging $253k receipts; 91 firms under 20 employees averaging $566k. | C, C5-S001 |

**Incumbent vs new entrant:**
- **Incumbent:** 38.6% operating margin at 800k-client scale.
- **New entrant:** pays platform fees, cannot price above SaaS by much, and carries E&O, crime and cyber insurance.
- **Retail benchmark:** a 15-employee contractor on QuickBooks Premium pays $88 + 15 × $13 = **$283/month** at retail.

## 5. Competition
**National players:**
- ADP, Paychex, Paycor (now Paychex).
- Gusto and QuickBooks Payroll (SaaS).
- PEOs.

**Construction-specific:**
- Construction ERP payroll (MISSING detail).
- Certified-payroll add-ons such as Points North (B).

**Embedded distribution:** payroll is also bundled with insurance. NEXT partners with QuickBooks and Gusto [B, C5-S025], and Paychex runs its own insurance agency [A, C5-S011].

**Customer complaints:** not collected this session (MISSING).

**Switching:** happens at quarter or year start and needs tax-history transfer. The friction protects the incumbent more than the entrant.

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** owner or office manager.
- **When they buy:** switching is usually timed to Jan 1 or a quarter start.
- **Spend:** about $150–$500/month for 5–25 employees [B/E].
- **Discovery:** through the CPA, bookkeeper, bank or insurance agent (Paychex: more than 50% via referral) [A].
- **Contract terms:** month-to-month (30-day termination is the incumbent norm) [A].

## 7. Failure evidence
- **Fiduciary failures are the classic bureau-killer.** A bureau that impounds client tax funds and misapplies them faces trust-fund liability, client penalties and fraud claims.
  - Specific Illinois enforcement examples were not retrieved this session: MISSING.
- **Bench precedent:** Employer.com (a payroll/HR player) bought Bench in a holiday weekend [C, C5-S006]. Consolidators buy distressed books cheaply, which caps exit values.

## 8. Regulation (Illinois and federal)
- No Illinois payroll-service licence was identified (still MISSING; the statute search was not completed).
- Whether a bureau that moves client wages and taxes is a money transmitter under Illinois law was **not verified** [E]. Mitigation: use a platform that is the payroll provider of record and holds the impound.
- IRS reporting-agent authorization (Form 8655) applies if the bureau files on clients' behalf (not fetched) [E].
- For prevailing-wage clients:
  - Certified payroll is due by the 15th monthly, per project [A, C5-S014, C5-S017].
  - SB1344 (effective 2025-06-30) makes "any officer, employee or agent whose job is to file" liable for civil penalties of up to $1,000 / $2,000 per missed month [C, C5-S019]. **A bureau that files is that agent.**

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **Wedge:** "construction payroll done for you." Job/class time capture, prevailing-wage rates, union fringes, certified payroll and WC pay-as-you-go.
- **Real, but:**
  - ADP plus Points North and construction ERPs already automate certified payroll for $175/month-class prices.
  - Payroll is a feature that bookkeeping clients will take from their bookkeeper only if it is cheap and error-free.
- **Copyability:** high.
- **Moat:** only service quality and the bundled relationship.

## 10. Economics sanity check (year 3) [E drivers; platform cost E/MISSING]
**Low:** 25 clients × $325 × 12 = $97.5k revenue.
- Costs: platform 25 × $100 × 12 = $30k; labor $32k (half a specialist); overhead $25k.
- **Operating profit about $10k.**

**Base:** 60 × $375 × 12 = $270k revenue.
- Costs: platform 60 × $110 × 12 = $79k; labor 1.5 specialists $97k; E&O, crime, cyber and marketing $38k.
- **Operating profit about $56k.**

**High:** 120 × $425 × 12 = $612k revenue.
- Costs: platform $173k; labor $195k; overhead $60k.
- **Operating profit about $184k.** Reaching 120 payroll clients by year 3 from zero is aggressive; Paychex-style referral networks took decades.

**Capital:** about $8–40k at risk, covering insurance, platform onboarding and year-1 losses.

**Payroll as an add-on:** inside a bookkeeping relationship, the incremental margin is about $250/client-month with no extra CAC. That is the only version worth keeping.
