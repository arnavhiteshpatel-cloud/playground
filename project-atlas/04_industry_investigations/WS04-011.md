# WS04-011 — Dental insurance verification, claims & AR recovery outsourcing for independent practices · Stage C

> **Verdict: DROP as a standalone business. Keep only as an optional cross-sell inside WS04-014.** · Revised evidence level: **D**.
>
> **Reason:**
> - **Low price ceiling.** Published offshore and remote competitors charge **$225–825 per office per month** for verification tiers on month-to-month terms, or **$2–8 per verification** (eAssist and others).
> - **Labor cost.** A U.S.-staffed entrant pays about $55k loaded per specialist, and each specialist serves only about 10 offices.
> - **Year-3 result.** Operating profit comes out between −$15k and +$140k, with a base case near $69k.
> - **Shrinking market.** The independent base in Illinois is shrinking, but slowly.
> - **No wedge.** The AI leverage on benefit look-ups and narratives is available to every competitor.
> - **Conclusion.** It is not a credible route to $250k a year.

## 1. Industry structure
- **Market base.** Illinois had 5,781 dental establishments in 2023 and 5,940 in 2019 (−2.7%) [C, C6-S003, C6-S004].
- **Practice structure.** 71.2% of Illinois dentists work in single-location practices, and 13.9% are DSO-affiliated (2025) [C, C6-S001].
- **DSO dynamics.** DSOs centralize billing in-house, so every DSO affiliation removes a potential customer.
- **Supply side.** Outsourced verification is a national remote market dominated by offshore and nearshore labor. Firm counts are MISSING.

## 2. Value chain & mechanism
- **Payment flow.** The practice pays a monthly tier or a per-verification fee. Aged-A/R recovery is sometimes billed as a contingency fee.
- **Where margin is earned.** The spread between labor cost and price. Offshore labor sets the price.
- **What the entrant would sell:**
  - Pre-visit eligibility and benefit breakdowns.
  - Claim submission with attachments.
  - A/R follow-up.
  - PPO credentialing.

## 4. Small-start evidence
- eAssist and many remote firms clearly exist and publish prices.
- Their founding scale is MISSING. No documented Illinois small-start case was found.

## 5. Competition
- **Main field:**
  - Offshore and nearshore verification firms.
  - eAssist (a U.S.-marketed remote firm).
  - Freelancers on Upwork.
- **Embedded alternatives:**
  - Practice-management-embedded eligibility tools.
  - Distributor RCM offerings.
- **DSO business offices.** They do this work in-house and remove customers from the market.
- **Switching costs.** Low. Contracts are month-to-month [C6-S009].
- **Customer complaints (D, not retrieved).** Errors in benefit breakdowns and communication lag.

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Who buys.** The office manager decides; the owner signs.
- **Triggers.** Front-desk turnover and rising write-offs.
- **How they trial.** A low-risk test such as an aged-A/R cleanup, or a single month.
- **Typical spend.** About $225–825/mo [D].
- **Where they find vendors:**
  - Study clubs.
  - Dental consultants.
  - Online search.
  - Practice-management marketplaces.

## 7. Failure evidence
- **Price race to the bottom** against offshore labor at $2–8 per verification [D, C6-S009].
- **Client loss** when practices join DSOs (+0.4 pp a year in Illinois [C6-S001, C6-S002]).
- **Dependence on practice-management system access** and on staff turnover.
- **No published failure statistics** were found (MISSING).

## 8. Regulation
- **HIPAA.** A BAA is required [A, WS04-S018].
- **Licensing.** No Illinois licence was identified.
- **Medicaid dental claims.** Agent fees cannot be percentage-based when the agent receives payment [A, C6-S035].
- **Collections.** Contingency A/R work collects from insurers, not patients. Patient collections could trigger the Illinois Collection Agency Act (not verified; avoid patient collections).

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **Proposed wedge.** Contingency aged-A/R cleanup as a no-risk trial, converting to a U.S.-based monthly service with dashboards.
- **Cost.** Specialist time. The cleanup is profitable only if recoveries are large.
- **Copyability.** Fully copyable.
- **Accumulation.** Weak. Payer knowledge is generic.
