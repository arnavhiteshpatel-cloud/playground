# WS04-014 — HIPAA-focused managed IT for independent dental, vet & small medical practices · Stage C

> **Verdict: ADVANCE to Stage D**, narrowed to "managed IT + HIPAA security-risk-analysis/compliance layer". This is the surviving core of SYN-002. Revised evidence level: **D**. Competitor prices are now documented (B list prices). Costs and win rates are still E.
>
> **Reason:**
> - Published dental-MSP prices are much lower than Stage A assumed: **$399–749 per practice per month**, against Stage A's $800–3,000. Year-3 profit therefore depends on volume: about 35 offices for a ~$120k base case.
> - The good news is that Illinois consolidation is slow. DSO affiliation rose only from 12.7% to 13.9% of Illinois dentists over 2022–25, against 12.7% to 17.0% nationally.
> - The founder fit is real.
> - Billing is prepaid and recurring.
> - It is the cheapest idea in this batch to validate.
>
> The wedge, local on-site depth plus imaging and practice-management software, is real but copyable.

## 1. Industry structure
- **Base.** Illinois had **5,781 dental** and **1,159 veterinary** establishments in 2023 [C, C6-S003]. Dental counts fell 2.7% from 5,940 in 2019, while vet counts rose 5.4% [C, C6-S004].
- **Fragmentation.** About 71% of Illinois dentists work in single-location practices: 31.2% solo and 40.0% multi-dentist. Only 8.4% sit in 100+ location organizations [C, C6-S001].
- **Consolidation pace.** Illinois DSO affiliation went from 12.7% (2022) to 13.9% (2025), about 0.4 points a year. Nationally it went from 12.7% to 17.0% [C, C6-S001, C6-S002].
- **Vets.** Corporates own about 25% of primary-care and about 75% of specialty/ER practices, and expansion is slowing [C, C6-S005].
- **Supply side.** The MSP side is huge and fragmented: 2,200 Illinois computer-systems-design establishments [C, C6-S003].
- **Market size.** The Chicago-metro share of practices is MISSING; we estimate 60–65% (E), or about 3,500–3,800 dental plus about 650 vet sites.

## 2. Value chain & mechanism
- **Who pays.** The practice owner pays a flat monthly fee, either per practice or per device. On top of that come projects (server replacement, new-office build-outs) and resold hardware.
- **Where margin sits.** In remote monitoring and automation leverage. On-site labor and imaging/sensor integration are the cost drivers.
- **What the entrant sells.** A local, dental-fluent tech team (Dentrix/Eaglesoft/Open Dental, Dexis/Schick sensors). The service includes a guaranteed on-site SLA plus a yearly HIPAA Security Rule risk analysis.
- **Required paperwork.** A BAA is required [A, WS04-S018].

- Their founding histories and revenues are MISSING. No documented "started from one client and grew" case was retrieved.

## 5. Competition
**National competitors:**
- Remote dental MSPs at $399–749 per practice.
- Distributor tech divisions, which carry equipment relationships.
- Practice-management vendors pushing cloud practice-management software (Dentrix Ascend, Curve). Cloud PM shrinks the server work.

**Local competitors:**
- Generalist Chicago MSPs.
- One-person "dental IT guys".

**Weaknesses of the competition:**
- Remote-first providers cap on-site visits (Siotek: 4 per year).
- Generalists lack imaging and sensor know-how.

**Switching costs:**
- Moderate: credentials, documentation, and a 12-month initial term are common [C6-S007].

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** the owner dentist signs. The office manager is the day-to-day buyer and gatekeeper.
- **Triggers:**
  - An outage.
  - A ransomware scare.
  - Opening a new office.
  - A practice purchase.
  - A failing server.
- **Contract:** monthly or annual. Typical spend is about $400–1,200/month plus projects, estimated from the published price ranges (E).
- **How vendors are found:**
  - Peers and study clubs.
  - Equipment dealer reps.
  - Practice brokers and accountants.
  - Search.

## 7. Failure evidence
- **Client loss from consolidation.** Illinois sees about 0.4 points a year of DSO affiliation and about 0.7% a year of establishment decline [C6-S001–S004]. Practice transitions where the buyer brings its own IT add to this. Estimated churn is 3–6% a year (E).
- **Margin compression** from flat-rate remote providers.
- **Breach liability** after a ransomware event at a client.
- **Underpricing on-site time.**
- No bankruptcy or enforcement data specific to dental MSPs was found (MISSING).

## 8. Regulation
- No Illinois licence is required for IT management.
- **HIPAA:** BAA, Security Rule safeguards and breach notification flow-down [A, WS04-S018].
- **Cabling:** Illinois or Chicago licensing for low-voltage or structured cabling is not verified (MISSING). Subcontract cabling to a licensed firm.
- **Insurance:** cyber and tech E&O (premiums MISSING, E: $5–12k/yr).

## 9. Entry wedge test
**Why a practice would switch:**
- Same-day on-site response within a 30–45-minute radius.
- Imaging and practice-management certified technicians.
- The HIPAA risk analysis and policies done for the practice, which remote flat-rate vendors sell only in custom tiers [C6-S007].
- A written ransomware-recovery time.

**Price:** at or slightly above remote MSPs, about $85 per workstation (E).

**Is it copyable?** Yes. Defensibility comes from local density (the technician's drive time), from dealer and broker referral relationships, and from documentation lock-in.
