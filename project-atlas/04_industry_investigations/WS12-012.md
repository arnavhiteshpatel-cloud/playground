## Industry structure

- **Philippine IT-BPM sector, 2025:** $40.3B revenue and 1.9M FTE. Forecast for 2026: $42.3B and 1.96M FTE [C, C9-S021].
- **2028 range:** $43.3–50.5B revenue and 1.85–2.14M FTE [C, C9-S021].
- **Emerging competing countries:** South Africa, Egypt, Poland, Colombia, Costa Rica and Vietnam [C, C9-S021].
- **Size of the SMB dedicated-staff/VA niche:** MISSING. Outsource Accelerator lists thousands of firms (D, search extract).
- **US Census proxy:** NAICS 561320 (temporary help) has 38,254 establishments. It is a poor proxy because offshore seats are not US payroll [C, C9-S007].
- **Cyclicality:** moderate. Offshore labor is partly counter-cyclical, because cost-cutting drives adoption.

## Value chain & mechanism

1. The US SMB pays a monthly fee per dedicated full-time worker.
2. The provider recruits and vets the worker.
3. The worker is employed or contracted abroad, either through the provider's own entity or an employer-of-record (EOR).
4. The provider supplies equipment, security and supervision, plus a US account manager.

**Where margin sits:** the spread between price and the all-in worker cost.

| Model | Monthly spread per seat | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Managed (typical) | ~$800–1,200 | E, derived from B |
| Marketplace (VirtualStaff.ph) | $99 | B |

**Who captures margin:** firms that own vertical training (insurance agency-management systems, dental billing) and retention.

## Small-start evidence

- **Agency VA:** founded 2018, now "hundreds of clients," promoted by a state insurance-agent association with a 10% member discount [D, C9-S020]. This shows a vertical-focused firm can grow through association channels. Revenue is not disclosed.
- **Other cases:** none verified.

## Competition

- **Players:** generalist VA agencies (Stealth, Wing, Cyberbacker, MyOutDesk), marketplaces (OnlineJobs.ph, VirtualStaff.ph), and vertical specialists that already hold association endorsements (Agency VA for insurance) [D, C9-S020].
- **Typical terms:** month-to-month after 90 days [D, C9-S020]. Switching costs are low.
- **Common customer complaints:** worker turnover, quality drift and time-zone gaps (D; reviews not systematically sampled).
- **Incumbents' weakness:** generalists lack vertical software proficiency. Marketplaces leave management to the client.

## Customer purchasing behavior

- **Decision-maker:** the owner or office manager of a 5–100 employee firm.
- **Purchase trigger:** a hiring difficulty or a cost-cutting moment.
- **Sales cycle:** 2–6 weeks, with a trial seat first.
- **Spend:** $1,600–3,000 per seat-month. Expansion comes from adding seats.
- **How they find providers:** association partner programs, peers, search and LinkedIn.

## Failure evidence

- **AI displacement:** IBPAP's downgrade is explicitly tied to AI and slower buyer decisions [C, C9-S021]. Robert Half warns that AI may let clients automate "in lieu of services" [A, WS12-S008].
- **Commoditization:** the $99/seat marketplace pricing [B, C9-S017].
- **Other failure modes (E):** worker poaching by clients, attrition, and data breaches in insurance and healthcare verticals.
- **Churn data:** MISSING.

## Regulation (Illinois + federal)

- **Illinois Private Employment Agency Act:** excludes recruiters compensated solely by employers [A, C9-S022]. The firm is the employer of the offshore staff, and it should not charge workers fees.
- **Illinois Day and Temporary Labor Services Act:** not applicable, because workers are not dispatched to Illinois worksites (analyst reading).
- **Worker country:** labor law applies. In the Philippines that means 13th-month pay and statutory contributions. An EOR partner can satisfy these.
- **Client data rules:** HIPAA business associate agreements for dental and medical clients, and GLBA safeguards for insurance and financial clients, call for security controls.
- **Licenses:** none, and no exam. Setup takes about 1–2 months.

## Entry wedge test

- **Wedge:** one vertical (e.g., independent P&C insurance agencies) with workers pre-trained on that vertical's agency-management systems, plus US account management and a replacement guarantee.
- **Delivery cost:** low.
- **Copyability:** high. Agency VA already occupies the insurance association channel.
- **Durable edges:** retention, training IP, and the ability to redeploy workers from automatable tasks to phone and judgment work as AI absorbs data entry.
