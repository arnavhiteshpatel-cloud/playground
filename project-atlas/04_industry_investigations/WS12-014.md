# WS12-014 — Contingency direct-hire recruiting desk in a technical niche

> **Verdict: ADVANCE**
>
> - **Revised evidence level:** C. Incumbent data is A, fees are D, and productivity is E.
> - **Year-3 operating profit:** $40–250k (base ≈ $150k before founder pay).
>
> **Regulatory barrier removed.** The Illinois Department of Labor reads the Private Employment Agency Act to exclude anyone in the business of recruiting "compensated solely by any employer" [A, C9-S022]. The assumed IDOL license, $5,000 bond and approved forms therefore do not apply to an employer-paid contingency desk. Feasibility rises to 10.
>
> **Demand is recovering.** Robert Half's permanent-placement revenue grew **6.2%** in Q3 2026 after falling 13.6% in 2025. Its perm segment operating margin was 11.1% [A, C9-S023; A, WS12-S008].
>
> **Fees.** The most common North American direct-hire fee is 20% [D, C9-S025], so a $110k technical hire yields about $22k.
>
> **Weaknesses:**
> - It is founder-dependent (G7 warning).
> - It is cyclical.
> - AI-enabled platforms expect fees on commoditized roles to compress [D, C9-S024].
>
> It survives because a technical founder can credibly assess hard-to-fill research, data or engineering candidates.

## Industry structure
- **Establishment counts (2023)** [C, C9-S007]:

  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 561311 | Employment placement agencies | 7,679 | 329,566 | 356 |
  | 561312 | Executive search | 5,897 | 38,564 | 329 |

  87% of executive-search establishments have fewer than 10 employees.
- **Fragmentation:** extreme. Boutique desks dominate the establishment count.
- **Cyclicality:** permanent placement is the first line cut in downturns (−13.6% at RH in 2025) and among the first to recover (+6.2% in Q3 2026) [A, WS12-S008, C9-S023].
- **Illinois:** the Chicago metro is a large technical-hiring market (analyst knowledge). Illinois counts are as in the table above.

## Value chain & mechanism
1. The employer gives a job order. It is non-exclusive and contingent.
2. The recruiter sources, screens and presents candidates, then manages interviews and the offer.
3. The fee is invoiced on the start date, net-30, with a 60–90-day guarantee or pro-rata refund [D, C9-S024, C9-S025].
4. Margin is almost all recruiter labor. RH perm gross margin is 99.8% [A, C9-S023].
5. Realization falls below billed fees because of fallouts. Kforce books direct hire net of a fallout reserve with a ≤90-day fallout period [A, WS12-S009].

## Operator economics
- **Robert Half perm placement (incumbent):**
  - Q3 2026 revenue $116.9M; gross margin 99.8%; segment operating margin 11.1% [A, C9-S023].
  - FY2025 operating margin was 2.9% at the cyclical trough [B, WS12-S007].
  - Takeaway: at scale, recruiter compensation and overhead consume almost everything.
- **Kforce Direct Hire:** $25.7M in FY2025 [A, WS12-S009].
- **Fee schedules:**
  - RH lists a 35% standard fee but agreed to 25% in a public contract [D, C9-S025].
  - The SIA 2021 survey found 20% most common, with direct-hire-only firms at 20–25% [D, C9-S025].
  - The gap between list and negotiated fees is the key realization fact.
- **Boutique owner-producer economics:** the fee less tools and insurance flows almost entirely to the owner. No boutique financials are disclosed (MISSING).

## Small-start evidence
- No named boutique with documented growth was verified this session.
- Paraform reports its curated recruiters fill roles in about 12 days at an average placed compensation of $260k [D, C9-S024]. This is a platform claim. It shows independent recruiters can win high-fee searches through marketplaces.

## Competition
- **Competitor types:**
  - national firms (RH, Kforce);
  - thousands of boutiques;
  - in-house talent-acquisition teams with AI sourcing;
  - recruiter marketplaces (Paraform, Dover) that pool job orders for independent recruiters.
- **Client complaints:** spammy candidate flooding, weak technical screening, and fees out of proportion to effort (D).
- **Switching costs:** nil; job orders are non-exclusive. Repeat business follows quality of hires.
- **Main threat:** AI-driven compression on commoditized roles. Paraform's own view is that finding people for mid-market roles "is no longer worth a 20% fee" [D, C9-S024].

## Customer purchasing behavior
- **Buyers:** hiring managers and HR at 50–2,000-employee firms.
- **Buying trigger:** a role open more than 45–60 days, or one that is niche or senior.
- **Selection:** they pick agencies through referrals, LinkedIn outreach and candidate-led marketing.
- **Terms:**
  - fee agreement signed per client;
  - typically 18–25% of first-year base;
  - 90-day guarantee;
  - 6–12-month candidate ownership [D, C9-S024].
- **Purchase frequency:** episodic, with 1–10 roles a year per client.

## Failure evidence
- **Ramp longer than founder runway:** the first placement typically takes 3–9 months (E).
- **Hiring freezes:** 2025 is a concrete example (−13.6%) [A, WS12-S008].
- **Fee disputes** when a client hires a presented candidate later.
- **Recruiters leaving with client relationships.**
- **Fallouts within guarantee periods** [A, WS12-S009].

## Regulation (Illinois + federal)
- **Private Employment Agency Act (225 ILCS 515):**
  - It excludes recruiting and consulting firms compensated solely by employers [A, C9-S022].
  - Charging candidates any fee, or placing domestic or theatrical workers, would bring the firm back under the Act. That means an annual IDOL license with a $250–500 fee and a $5,000 bond [A, WS12-S015].
  - The statute text could not be read (ILGA unavailable). The founder should confirm with counsel at launch.
- **Pay rules:** federal and Illinois anti-discrimination law. The Illinois Equal Pay Act requires pay-scale disclosure in job postings by employers. Whether that duty extends to agencies' postings is not verified (MISSING).
- **Other requirements:** no exam, no bond and no license. Operations can start in weeks.

## Entry wedge test
- **Wedge:** the founder's technical and research background allows real screening of lab, QA, data-engineering and manufacturing-engineering candidates. Generalist agencies and AI sourcing do this poorly.
- **Delivery:** fast shortlists of 3–5 deeply vetted candidates, and an 18–20% introductory fee to win first clients.
- **Defensibility:** partly copyable. The niche network and placed-candidate referrals compound into the real moat.
