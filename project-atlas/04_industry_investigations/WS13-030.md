# WS13-030 — Low-cost direct cremation provider (online arrangements, employed licensed funeral director, trade crematory)

> **Verdict: HOLD (low priority — advance only with a licensed funeral-director co-founder)** · revised evidence level **D** (unchanged) · 2026-11-28
>
> **Licensing.** Illinois law lets an **unlicensed owner own and manage** a funeral business. But the at-need arrangement itself must be made by a licensee, and unlicensed staff may remove a body only when a licensed funeral director accompanies them on the call (an intern may remove alone) [A, C8-S008]. Licensure takes mortuary-science education, an Illinois internship and the National Board exam [A, C8-S009]. The founder cannot obtain it within ~12 months, so the business carries one or two licensed salaries from the first case.
>
> **Prices.** The Chicago price umbrella is wide:
> - SCI's Drake & Son: direct cremation **$5,290–$6,485** [B, C8-S011]
> - Independent "affordable boutique" Inclusive Funeral Care: **$2,200–$2,737 plus a $300 cremation charge** [B, C8-S010]
> - Online providers: advertised from **$995** in Illinois [D, C8-S013]
>
> SCI holds ~18% of North American deathcare revenue and owns the discount brands Neptune Society, National Cremation Society and Trident [A, C8-S012]. Price competition therefore comes from the incumbent too, not only from start-ups.
>
> Break-even needs roughly 300–650 cases a year depending on price. Rebuilt year-3 operating profit is **−$75k to $230k, base ~$60k**. The structural tailwind (cremation 62.8% → 69.1% by 2030 [C, WS13-S017]) and prepaid cash are real. Customer-acquisition cost and licensed labor decide the outcome.

## 2. Industry structure
- **Illinois (2022).** 570 funeral-home firms (766 establishments), $792M receipts, 4,238 employees. 300 firms have fewer than 5 employees. The five 500+-employee enterprises operate 55 Illinois establishments with $68M of receipts [C, C8-S005].
- **U.S.** 10,725 firms [C, C8-S005].
- **Incumbents.** SCI operates 1,485 funeral locations and 500 cemeteries and says most deathcare businesses are local independents [A, C8-S012]. Carriage Services averaged **$5,763 revenue per funeral contract** in 2025 [A, WS13-S016].
- **Demand.** Deaths are demographic and non-cyclical. Cremation share keeps rising [C, WS13-S017; A, WS13-S015], and direct cremation is the low-price end of that shift.
- **Missing.** Cook County annual deaths (IDPH vital statistics not retrieved). Park Lawn's current ownership was not verified this session.

## 3. Value chain & mechanism
The family pays the funeral business a package price before service. Out of that, the business pays:
- a trade crematory (licensed under the Illinois Crematory Regulation Act; Comptroller oversight strengthened by HB 4695 from 2027-01-01 [A, WS13-S020])
- removal staff
- cash-advance items: death certificates (~$40 each at IFC [B, C8-S010]), permits, medical-examiner fees (MISSING)
- container and urn costs

Margin sits in the arrangement and service fee. Incumbents also earn on merchandise, ceremonies and **preneed trust funds**; preneed sales are regulated by the Comptroller under the Funeral or Burial Funds Act, details MISSING. The new entrant would capture demand online and by phone, have a licensee make the arrangement, and outsource cremation.

## 7. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** next of kin, at time of death (hours to days), via phone or online search.
- **Referral sources:** hospice social workers and hospitals.
- **Frequency:** no repeat, except through preneed and referrals.
- **Pre-need buyers** compare prices months ahead; that requires a Comptroller preneed licence, details MISSING.
- **Search cost:** Google CPCs for "cremation near me" are high (D, not measured this session).

## 8. Failure evidence
- **Regulatory risk is concentrated in custody and handling.** Illinois strengthened crematory oversight after scandals, with HB 4695 adding inspection powers, penalties up to $10,000 per violation, summary suspension and SOP requirements [A, WS13-S020].
- **Curbstoning.** IDFPR warns publicly against unlicensed "curbstone" funeral directing [A, WS13-S036].
- **Business failure modes:**
  - CAC inflation
  - price wars started by incumbent discount brands
  - paying licensed staff while volume ramps
  - dependence on one trade crematory

## 9. Regulation (precise)
- **225 ILCS 41/1-15** [A, C8-S008]:
  - removal of bodies and at-need arrangements are funeral directing, and arrangements must be made by licensees
  - unlicensed helpers must be accompanied by a licensed funeral director "on all calls"; interns may remove alone
  - transport to final disposition is under the immediate supervision of a licensee
  - an unlicensed owner may own and manage the establishment if the owner does not practise
- **Licensure** (IDFPR 034): Illinois internship, mortuary-science program, Conference National Board exam [A, C8-S009]. IDFPR lists licence types for funeral director, funeral director & embalmer and intern; no separate establishment licence appears on the profession page [A, WS13-S036]. Whether an establishment registration or address of record is required is MISSING.
- **Crematory Regulation Act.** Only the trade crematory is licensed. HB 4695 tightens oversight from 2027 [A, WS13-S020].
- **FTC Funeral Rule.** A General Price List must be provided (see GPLs above).
- **Preneed.** Comptroller licence (MISSING); avoid initially.

## 10. Entry wedge test
- **Proposed wedge:** transparent all-in price, roughly $1,495–$2,195 (about half of the boutique all-in and a third of SCI), online paperwork, and status updates.
- **Cost:** search marketing plus hospice outreach.
- **Copyable:** yes. SCI's discount brands and online firms already sell exactly this.
- **What accumulates:** reviews, hospice and facility referral relationships, and preneed contracts (if licensed later).

## 11. Economics sanity check (year 3, E unless noted)
**Per-case direct cost (~$850):**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Trade crematory | $300 (E, anchored on IFC's $300 line [B]) |
| Removal (outsourced) | $350 |
| Container | $60 |
| Holding/refrigeration | $75 |
| Supplies | $20 |
| Card fees | ~$45 |

**Fixed costs (~$195k):** licensed FD $85k loaded, second licensee/intern for 24/7 coverage $60k, office $30k, insurance $10k, software $10k. Cash-advance items are passed through.

| Case | Price × cases | Contribution after CAC | Op. profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | $1,495 × 400 | ($1,495 − $850 − $350 CAC) = $295 × 400 = $118k | 118 − 195 = **−$77k** |
| Base | $1,895 × 400 | ($1,895 − $850 − $350) = $695 × 400 = $278k | 278 − 195 − 25 (merchandise/misc) ≈ **$60k** |
| High | $2,195 × 500, in-house removals (−$150/case, +$50k staff) | ($2,195 − $700 − $350) = $1,145 × 500 = $573k | 573 − 195 − 50 − 100 (third licensee, larger office) ≈ **$230k** |

- **Break-even** ≈ 281 cases at $695 contribution and 660 cases at $295.
- **Capital at risk:** $40–100k (office, refrigeration or holding arrangements, removal vehicle, licensed salaries before volume, marketing).
- **Cash:** families prepay, so cash conversion is excellent once volume exists.
