# WS14-008 — Retailer/distributor deduction & chargeback recovery for emerging CPG brands

> **Verdict: HOLD** · Revised evidence level: **D** (up from E; fee levels still MISSING) · Year-3 operating profit **$0–150k** (base ≈ $70–85k)
>
> The pain is real. Vendors claim deductions run **1–5% of gross revenue** and **30–60% are invalid** [D, C9-S009]. Small natural-channel brands face short dispute windows: UNFI ~30–60 days, KeHE ~180 days [D, C9-S011].
>
> The Stage A thesis was "an AI-leveraged service for brands without analysts." It now meets a crowded field:
> - **SPS Commerce**, the public EDI network that most retailer suppliers already use, sells Revenue Recovery for Walmart, Amazon, Kroger, Target, UNFI and KeHE. It leads with a free audit and claims $2B+ recovered [D, C9-S010].
> - **AI-native funded entrants** (Confido, Glimpse) and small services (Claire List) target exactly the same emerging brands [D, C9-S009, C9-S011].
>
> No competitor publishes a contingency rate, so the 15–30% fee assumption is untested. It is plausible as a channel-specialist service, but the AI wedge is no longer differentiating.

## Industry structure
- **Market size:** no official count of supplier-side deduction-management firms.
- **Census proxy:** NAICS 541219 ("other accounting services") shows 47,699 U.S. establishments (92% with fewer than 10 employees) and 1,759 in Illinois [C, C9-S007]. It is a very loose proxy.
- **Demand drivers:**
  - every shipment to big-box, grocery and natural-channel distributors generates deductions (shortages, compliance fines, promotions/MCB, pricing);
  - retailers' own recovery auditors (PRGX) generate post-audit claims against suppliers [D, WS14-S026].
- **Cyclicality:** low. Deductions scale with shipments, and retailer cost pressure arguably increases them in downturns.
- **Illinois:** Chicago is a large CPG hub (analyst knowledge). Brand counts are MISSING.

## Value chain & mechanism
1. The retailer or distributor short-pays an invoice.
2. The brand must download remittance and backup from the portal, classify the deduction, and match proof (POD/BOL, promo agreement, ASN).
3. The brand disputes the deduction within the window and tracks the credit memo or repayment.

Under the contingency model, the provider is paid a share of the cash recovered. Recovery arrives as repayment or offsets, often 30–120 days later (E).

Margin sits in analyst productivity: deductions handled per hour and win rate. That is exactly what software (SPS, Confido) automates.

## Operator economics
- **SPS Commerce (public EDI incumbent):**
  - recovery coverage of about 11 retailers and distributors;
  - free audit; "$2B+" recovered claim;
  - case claim: Owlet recovered $1.4M in six months [D, C9-S010].
  - Pricing, and the recovery business's segment economics, are not disclosed (MISSING).
- **Confido:** AI deduction management, auto-disputes and cash application for CPG [D, C9-S009]. Pricing not shown.
- **Glimpse:** low monthly fee plus commission on recoveries (rate not published) [D, C9-S011].
- **Claire List:** third-party UNFI deduction service for small food and beverage brands; fees not shown (search extract, not fetched).
- **PRGX (retailer side):** 300+ enterprise clients and about $1.8B in annual cash-flow claims [D, WS14-S026]. This is the counterparty, not a comparable.
- **Gross and operating margins of supplier-side services:** MISSING.

## Small-start evidence
Claire List and Glimpse appear to be young firms serving emerging brands. Their founding dates and revenue were not verified, so no documented small-start growth case exists.

## Competition
- **Software platforms:** SPS, HighRadius (enterprise), Vividly, Promomash, Confido.
- **Services:** broker-run deduction desks, outsourced AR firms, freelancers.
- **SPS's structural advantage:** it already holds the brand's EDI and ASN data, which is the proof needed to win shortage disputes.
- **Incumbent weaknesses:**
  - SaaS tools still require a brand employee to run them;
  - natural-channel distributor rules (UNFI/KeHE codes, MCB fees such as KeHE's 8% on MCB with a $65 minimum per DC) are idiosyncratic [D, C9-S011].
- **Switching costs:** modest. Portal credentials and document-retrieval setup take weeks.

## Customer purchasing behavior
- **Decision-maker:** founder/CEO, fractional CFO or controller of $5–100M brands.
- **Trigger:** a cash squeeze or a large unexplained short-pay.
- **Purchase pattern:** often starts with a retroactive audit, which is free at SPS and therefore no longer a wedge.
- **Contract terms:** monthly retainer plus contingency, or contingency only. Terms are MISSING.
- **How brands find providers:** brokers, 3PLs, fractional CFOs, peer communities (D).

## Failure evidence
- **Brands too small:** a $3M brand with 3% deductions and 35% invalid has about $31k a year of invalid deductions. At 60% recovery and a 20% fee, that is ≈$3.8k of revenue per client per year (E arithmetic). It is not serviceable profitably by hand.
- **Expired windows:** UNFI's 30–60 days makes retroactive recovery small.
- **Brand failure and consolidation** in emerging CPG (no data).
- **Portal terms of service** restricting automated access: MISSING.

## Regulation (Illinois + federal)
- **Licensing:** none found. This is not debt collection: the brand is disputing amounts owed to it, not collecting consumer debts.
- **Contract terms:** engagement letters should specify the fee base (cash received vs. credit issued).
- **Data access:** portal-access authorization is via client credentials. Retailer terms are MISSING.
- **Bonds or exams:** none.
- **Feasibility:** high.

## Entry wedge test
Possible wedges:
- channel specialization (UNFI/KeHE natural channel, or regional grocers SPS covers less);
- a done-for-you service rather than software;
- fixed monthly pricing for brands under $20M.

It costs little to deliver (one trained analyst plus scripts). It is copyable, and SPS can bundle recovery with EDI at near-zero acquisition cost.

The only durable edge would be relationships with brokers and 3PLs who refer clients. That is not testable without contact.
