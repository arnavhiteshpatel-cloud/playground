|---|---|

| Reason | The mandate is real, recurring and now more punitive. Every contractor and subcontractor on Illinois public works must file certified payroll by the 15th of each month through IDOL's portal [A, C5-S014, C5-S018]. SB1344 (effective 2025-06-30) adds civil penalties of up to $1,000/$2,000 per missed month [C, C5-S019], on top of 4-year debarment exposure [A, C5-S016]. But this is a *report generated from payroll data*, and payroll platforms already sell it as an add-on for $175/month + $7.50/report [B, C5-S013]. The standalone ticket is low and seasonal. The penalty also attaches to "any … agent whose job is to file", which shifts liability onto an outsourced filer. Standalone year-3 base is about $50k, versus about $146k when attached to bookkeeping. |

## 1. Industry structure (mandate and buyer pool)
- **Statute** (820 ILCS 130/5) [A, C5-S014]:
  - Applies to "any contractor and each subcontractor who participates in public works".
  - Filing is monthly, by the 15th for the prior month, and only for months with work.
  - Records must be kept 5 years.
  - Willful failure or false filing is a Class A misdemeanor.
  - A GC may rely on a sub's certification.
- **IDOL portal** [A, C5-S017, C5-S018]:
  - Requires an Illinois Public ID.
  - Uses a fixed-column Excel import template (new from 2025-07-01).
  - Payroll must be filed **per project**.
  - Filing for **two companies requires separate log-in accounts**.
  - Veteran and race/ethnicity fields are required.
- **Volume:**
  - A search snippet attributes about 1.5M portal submissions since 2020 to an IDOL release. The page body did not render, so this is unverified [D, C5-S034].
  - The number of contractors filing: **MISSING**.
  - Buyer universe proxy: 29,457 Illinois construction establishments, of which 18,685 are specialty trades [C, C5-S002]. The public-works share is unknown.
- **Growth drivers** (from WS15, not re-verified): lead-service-line replacement, school and water capital, and Illinois Shines prevailing-wage solar.
- **Funding risk:** IIJA authorization status after FY2026 is **MISSING**.

## 2. Value chain and mechanism
1. The contractor's payroll system calculates wages.
2. Someone maps hours to project, classification and county rate, then reconciles fringes (union fund or plan contributions).
3. That person completes the IDOL template per project and signs the certification.
4. The GC or public body checks the filing.

Agency-side tools (LCPtracker) are paid by owners and agencies, not subs (not verified this session).

The outsourced service captures the labor of steps 2–3. That labor shrinks as payroll platforms automate it.

## 5. Competition
**Competitors:**
- Payroll majors with certified-payroll modules or marketplace apps (B).
- Construction ERP payroll (not verified).
- Construction CPAs and bookkeepers who already file for clients.
- Union-side payroll services for signatory contractors (not verified).

**Weaknesses:**
- Small non-union subs newly winning public work (LSL, solar, school work) often run QuickBooks or Gusto, which lack the IDOL format.
- Their pain shows up as GC pay-application holds.

**Switching costs:** low. The service is easy to bring in-house once a template exists.

## 6. Customer purchasing behavior
- **Trigger:** the first prevailing-wage award, or a GC withholding payment for missing payrolls.
- **Decision-maker:** the owner.
- **Cadence:** monthly while public jobs are active, so the work is seasonal and lumpy.
- **Discovery:** GC referrals, the CPA, the surety agent, and public award lists (WS15).
- **Contracts:** monthly retainer or per-report [E].

## 7. Failure evidence
- **Liability falls on the filer** [C, C5-S019]:
  - Penalties run up to $1,000 for a first offense and $2,000 for later ones, and each missed month counts separately.
  - The penalty applies to the officer, employee or **agent** responsible for filing.
  - Two violations in 5 years can trigger debarment.
- **Debarment stakes** [A, C5-S016]:
  - A conviction under Section 5 means automatic 4-year debarment.
  - An error by the service can debar the client, an existential E&O exposure for a small firm.
- **Underpayment penalties** [A, C5-S015]: 20% (50% for repeat violations) payable to IDOL, plus monthly punitive damages. Classification mistakes propagate into wage liabilities.

## 8. Regulation
- No licence is needed to prepare reports.
- **Payroll processing:** if the service also processes payroll, see the WS02-026 issues (Form 8655, money transmission: unverified).
- **Legal advice:** giving worker-classification legal opinions risks unauthorized practice of law. Keep the service to data preparation from client-approved classifications [E].
- **E&O:** essential; cost MISSING.

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **Offer:** "No-hold certified payroll." Monthly filing by the 10th, rate lookups, a fringe reconciliation, and an audit binder.
- **Sold to:** small non-union subs named on public award or sub lists.
- **Why a buyer switches:**
  - GC payment holds;
  - SB1344 penalties;
  - QuickBooks/Gusto lack IDOL output.
- **Cost to deliver:** about 1–2 specialist-hours per report [E].
- **Copyability:** high. Any payroll vendor can add the template, and IDOL's single Excel template lowers the skill barrier for everyone, including DIY.
