# WS18-032 — Drain cleaning & sewer camera inspection service

> **Verdict: HOLD, with the G2_LICENSE warning kept. Do not advance until the statutory definition of "plumbing" in 225 ILCS 320/2–3 has been read.** Revised evidence level: **D**. The incumbent's figures are B-level, but new-entrant pricing and costs are only estimates (E).
>
> **Demand is real.** Roto-Rooter's revenue is about $900M a year, with a 21–23% adjusted EBITDA margin [B].
>
> **The deciding question is licensing, and it is still open:**
> - The founder can never hold an Illinois plumbing licence, which requires an apprenticeship of at least 4 years [A, WS05-S029].
> - Chicago separately requires a Drain Layer Contractor License to rod, clean, line or camera-inspect sewers outside a building [A via search snippet, C2-S030].
> - Root-killing chemicals need an IDOA "sewer line root control" licence category [A, C2-S026].
>
> **What the answer means for profit:**
> - If in-building rodding counts as plumbing, every job needs a licensed plumber (Chicago median wage $103k). Modelled year-3 profit then falls to about zero.
> - If it does not count as plumbing, year-3 profit is about $70k–$200k.
>
> **Lead costs are rising.** Roto-Rooter lost 477 basis points of margin in Q4 2025, partly on paid leads [B/D].

## 1. Industry structure
- **No separate Census code.** Drain cleaning falls inside NAICS 238220 (plumbing and HVAC): 107,004 US firms and $306B in receipts [C, C2-S012]. Illinois has 4,113 such firms, 2,608 of them with fewer than 5 employees [C, C2-S013].
- **Roto-Rooter** is the best-documented focused operator [B, C2-S028]:
  - Q4 2025 revenue: $220.6M, down 3.7%.
  - Branch residential revenue: $155.6M. Within that, drain cleaning fell 3.2% while plumbing grew 6.3%.
  - Commercial drain cleaning grew 2.0%.
  - Full-year 2025 revenue was about $900M, flat on 2024.
- **Demand drivers:** emergencies (non-discretionary) and older housing stock. In Chicago, demand is concentrated in pre-war bungalows and 2-flats (E).

## 2. Value chain and mechanism
- **Who pays:** the homeowner or landlord, per call, typically same day.
- **Margin capture:** the call fee plus upsells:
  - camera inspection;
  - hydro-jetting;
  - root maintenance plans;
  - referrals into excavation and sewer repair, which is licensed work and the most valuable.
- **Lead source:** mostly paid search, Google Local Services Ads and home-warranty networks.
- **What the entrant would do:** run technician vans with rodding machines, cameras and jetters, plus dispatch.

## 3. Operator economics
**Incumbent economics (Roto-Rooter / Chemed)** [B, C2-S028]:
- Q4 2025 gross margin 49.8% (51.3% a year earlier).
- Adjusted EBITDA margin 21.5%, down 477 bp.
- Full-year 2025 adjusted EBITDA margin about 22.7%, versus about 26.3% in 2024. This is an analyst calculation; verify against the filing.
- 2026 guidance: 22.5–23.0%.

**Lead costs:** management attributed the decline to paid-lead growth of 9.4% while natural leads fell [D, C2-S029].

**Labour costs (Chicago, BLS 2025):** plumbers have a median wage of $103,380, versus about $46–50k for unlicensed field trades [C, C2-S022, C2-S021].

**Small-operator economics:** MISSING. No Item 19 for Mr. Rooter or similar was retrieved, and BizBuySell was blocked.

## 4. Small-start evidence
None was documented this session (MISSING). Roto-Rooter has an independent contractor/franchise channel ($16.7M in Q4 revenue) [B, C2-S028]. That shows small operators work under the brand, but gives no figures for them.

## 5. Competition
- Roto-Rooter (company branches and franchisees).
- Mr. Rooter and other Neighborly brands.
- Every licensed plumbing contractor. Illinois has 4,113 plumbing/HVAC firms [C].
- Sewer contractors holding Chicago drain-layer licences.

**Effect on an entrant:** plumbers can offer rodding together with the repairs it reveals. An unlicensed drain specialist cannot do the repair work, so most of the job value goes to the plumber.

## 6. Customer purchasing behaviour
- **Emergency buyers** (backups) choose within hours, through search or a referral. Price sensitivity is low in the moment, but reviews matter.
- **Pre-purchase camera inspections** come through home inspectors and realtors (E).
- **Landlords** may buy recurring root-cutting plans (E).
- **Typical tickets** are estimates only (E): $250–450 per rodding call, $250–400 per camera inspection, $500–1,000 per jetting job.

## 7. Failure evidence
- Roto-Rooter's margin compression from lead costs and price concessions: implicit price concessions and credit memos rose by about $4M, or 57% [D, C2-S029 summary]. This is what an incumbent with a national brand faces. A newcomer buying the same leads would fare worse.
- No bankruptcy or enforcement data was found for drain operators (MISSING).
- Equipment damage and sewage-exposure claims are a likely risk (E).

## 8. Regulation
- **Illinois Plumbing License Law (225 ILCS 320):**
  - All plumbing must be performed by licensed plumbers.
  - Anyone performing plumbing for hire must register as a plumbing contractor [A, WS05-S029; C2-S031].
  - Whether clearing a stoppage or rodding a building drain counts as "plumbing" or is exempt **could not be verified**. The statute was unreachable (ilga.gov 503 and timeouts).
  - The Illinois Plumbing Code defers the definition of "plumbing" to the Act [A, C2-S031].
- **Chicago Drain Layer Contractor License:** required to construct, repair, rod, clean, line or camera-inspect sewers outside a building. Licensed plumbing contractors are exempt [A, C2-S030]. Requirements, bond and insurance are MISSING (page returned 403).
- **IDOA:** root-control herbicides require a commercial applicator licence in the "sewer line root control" category, at $300 for 3 years [A, C2-S026].
- **Home Repair Act:** lists sewers. Work on residences over $500 triggers its contract and insurance duties [A, WS18-S010].
- **Feasibility for this founder:** 4 (unresolved). The workaround of employing a licensed plumber is legal but changes the economics.

## 9. Entry wedge test
- **Wedge:** camera-first diagnosis, transparent pricing, and video reports for landlords and inspectors.
- **Copyability:** fully copyable. Every plumbing firm owns a camera.
- **Lead costs:** the wedge does not solve the paid-lead problem. Referral channels (home inspectors) are the only cheaper source.
