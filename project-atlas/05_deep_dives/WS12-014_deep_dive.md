# WS12-014 — Contingency direct-hire desk for controls, automation & I&C engineers in Illinois: Stage D deep dive (batch DG)

> **Verdict: FINALIST (conditional; G7 founder-dependence WARN).** Evidence level **C**: demand and pay are D (public postings), fee mechanics D/A, conversion E. Researched 2026-11-28.
>
> - **Niche chosen: controls, automation and instrumentation & controls (I&C) engineers.** This means PLC/HMI, SCADA/DCS, robotics and motion control. Adjacent electrical/process engineers are a second tier.
> - **Why this niche fits a tech/research founder:**
>   - The work is software-like and technically assessable.
>   - Pay is high: Illinois postings show **$74–157k** [D, DG-S034].
>   - Demand is evidenced by employers' own career systems.
> - **Named demand: 27 Illinois employers with current controls/automation/I&C or adjacent postings**, for example:
>   - **Sargent & Lundy:** 20+ I&C/automation engineer requisitions [D, DG-S020].
>   - **30+ days open:** Ingredion (Bedford Park), Medline (Mundelein), Abbott (North Chicago) and Primient (Decatur) [D, DG-S021/S025/S026/S029].
>   - **Several concurrent roles:** Yaskawa Waukegan and Formic Chicago [D, DG-S023/S024].
> - **Decisive fact:** no Illinois licence is needed for an employer-paid desk [A, C9-S022], and a $105k hire at 20% is a $21k fee [D, C9-S025]. **One placement covers a year of tools.**
> - **The countervailing facts:**
>   - Large employers channel agencies through vendor portals. Abbott runs a separate Workday "Agency" site [D, DG-S026].
>   - National staffing firms (Actalent, Aerotek) already post Illinois controls roles [D, DG-S034].
> - **Model results:**

## 1. Specific initial niche
**Contingency direct-hire search for controls, automation and I&C engineers (≈$90–160k base) at three types of employer:**
1. Illinois control-system integrators and automation OEMs: Wunderlich-Malec, Barry-Wehmiller Design Group, WESA, Concentric, Daifuku/Wynright, Yaskawa, Formic.
2. Mid-size and large process plants: Ingredion, Primient, Medline, Novolex, Mondelez, Conagra.
3. Engineering consultancies: Sargent & Lundy, Baxter & Woodman, CDM Smith.

**Geography:** Chicago metro and the I-39/I-74 manufacturing corridor (Rockford, Peoria/Morton, Normal, Decatur).

**Terms:**
- Fee 20% (18% introductory for the first role per client).
- 90-day replacement guarantee.
- 12-month candidate ownership.
- Invoice on start, net 30.

## 6. Customer acquisition
- **Customer #1 (target: fee agreement within 60 days, first start within 6 months):**
  1. Use the public ATS endpoints to list Illinois controls roles open 30+ days at Tier A/B employers.
  2. Recruit 2–3 strong Illinois controls engineers who are open to a move (LinkedIn/Recruiter Lite).
  3. Present a specific, anonymised candidate to each hiring manager (candidate-led marketing).
  4. Sign a 20% fee agreement per role.
- **Clients #2–10:**
  - Repeat roles at the first clients; Sargent & Lundy-type employers with many requisitions.
  - Referrals from placed engineers.
  - ISA (International Society of Automation) Chicago-section events (cost MISSING).
- **Clients #11–100:**
  - Junior recruiter/researcher once revenue passes $20k/month; second recruiter at $45k/month.
  - Expand to Wisconsin, Indiana and Iowa integrators.
  - Consider contract staffing in the same niche later (requires payroll float).
- **Funnel (base, E):**
  - 9 hiring-manager conversations about a live role per month → 30% sign a job order → 20% fill = **0.54 new paying clients/month**.
  - Active clients add ~0.45 repeat placements/year.
  - Year 3 ≈ 13 placements.
- **Unit economics:**
  - CAC ≈ $400 per new client plus ~$300/month ads.
  - First-fee contribution ≈ $17k, so payback is immediate on the first placement.
  - LTV ≈ $17.9k + 20 months × $670 ≈ $31k.
- **Weekly sales work:** about 15–20 hours of business development and 25–30 hours of sourcing, screening and closing. Recruiting is both the sales and the delivery.

## 7. Pricing strategy
- **Market evidence:**
  - 20% is the most common North American direct-hire fee; direct-hire-only firms charge 20–25% [D, C9-S025].
  - Platforms cite 15–25% with 60–90-day guarantees [D, C9-S024].
  - Robert Half's 35% list price is cut to 25% in public contracts [D, C9-S025].
- **Proposal:**
  - 20% standard; 22–25% for senior I&C or relocation searches.
  - 18% introductory on the first role.
  - Never below 18%. Discount with a two-role exclusive rather than a lower percentage.
  - Guarantee: replacement only (no cash refund) within 90 days.

## 8. Working capital and cash conversion
- No inventory and no payroll float. Cash cycle ≈ DSO 40 days (base); conservative 55.

| Active clients | Monthly revenue | Receivables |

| 50 (≈4 producers) | ≈ $87k | ≈ $116k |
| 200 (≈12–15 producers) | ≈ $350k | ≈ $465k |

- At 50 clients and above, structure recruiter commissions to be paid on collection, which offsets receivables.
- The real working-capital need is **founder runway during the 4–9-month ramp.** Living costs are excluded from the model; peak business funding is $11k (base) and $53k (conservative, including draws).

## 9. Regulatory path
1. **Illinois LLC and EIN.** The Secretary of State fee was not fetched (MISSING).
2. **No IDOL Private Employment Agency licence** when compensated solely by employers [A, C9-S022]. Never charge candidates. Charging candidates, or placing domestic or theatrical workers, triggers the annual licence ($250–500 plus a $5,000 bond) [A, WS12-S015].
   - Confirm with counsel; the statute text on ilga.gov was unavailable.
3. **Pay-scale disclosure (unverified, E):**
   - Illinois Equal Pay Act amendments (from 2025) require pay-scale and benefits disclosure in postings by employers with 15+ employees.
   - Third parties posting for them reportedly carry obligations too. Not verified: IDOL FAQ URLs returned 404 and ilga.gov was blocked.
4. **Anti-discrimination:** IL Human Rights Act; federal Title VII; ADEA.
5. **Contracts:** a written fee agreement per client covering candidate ownership, guarantee and payment terms.
6. **Insurance:** professional liability/E&O ≈ $1.5–3k/yr and GL ≈ $500/yr. No quotes; E.

## 10. Operations workflow
1. Role intelligence (ATS endpoints; scripts reusable from this research).
2. Hiring-manager call.
3. Fee agreement.
4. Intake.
5. Sourcing (LinkedIn, referrals, ISA, alumni of integrators).
6. Technical screen. The founder runs a PLC/SCADA scenario interview and reviews code and project samples.
7. Submittal of 2–3 vetted candidates.
8. Interview coordination.
9. Offer and counter-offer management.
10. Start, invoice, 30/60/90-day check-ins, collection.

**Human vs AI vs automatable:**
- **Human:** persuasion, technical judgment, closing.
- **AI-assisted:** outreach drafting, interview notes, résumé redaction, market maps (≈$500/month of tools).
- **Automatable:** CRM and invoicing. Software and AI tools are ≈$475/month in the model; tripling them reduces year-3 operating profit by ~$12k.

## 11. Competitive position and defensibility
**What accumulates:**
- a proprietary map of every Illinois controls engineer and who employs them;
- placed-candidate goodwill (each becomes a future hiring manager or referrer);
- repeat-client relationships at integrators.

**What does not protect the business:**
- the fee agreement itself, since orders are non-exclusive;
- AI sourcing, which commoditises easy roles [D, C9-S024].

**Defensibility comes from niche depth and speed.** It is only partly transferable to hired recruiters. Recruiters can leave with relationships, hence the G7 warning and a delegation score of 4.

## 13. Validation plan
1. **Role map (≈$0, 1 week).** Rerun the ATS scripts weekly for 4 weeks.
   - **Success:** ≥40 Illinois controls/I&C engineer roles at ≥20 employers, ≥30% open 30+ days.
   - **Kill:** <15 roles, or most at gated large employers.
2. **Candidate supply test (Recruiter Lite trial, ≈$0–180, 3 weeks).** Approach 60 Illinois controls engineers.
   - **Success:** ≥20% reply and ≥5 open to a move at ≥$100k.
   - **Kill:** <8% reply.
3. **Fee-agreement test (founder time, 60 days).** 30 hiring-manager conversations with a specific candidate.
   - **Success:** ≥5 signed agreements at ≥18%.
   - **Kill:** <2, or all demand ≤15%.
4. **First fee (≤6 months).**
   - **Success:** one paid placement of ≥$15k collected.
   - **Kill:** none by month 7. Stop before any hire.
5. **Split-network test (Top Echelon $350 + $150/month, optional).** Measures whether controls job orders are available to work while building your own clients.
   - **Kill:** fewer than 3 relevant orders in 60 days.
