> **Verdict: RESERVE.** This is a sound, cheap, well-evidenced small business, but not a finalist.
>
> **Scores:** revised adjusted score **4.96** (raw 4.95, evidence C), against 5.27 at Stage C.
>
> **The decisive fact: the public market prices openly but buys in the most expensive way to serve.**
> - Public pond contracts carry clear prices: about **$1,000–$2,900 per pond per year** for biweekly programs (Wheaton, Mokena, Hawthorn Woods, Vernon Hills) and **$8,000 per pond for weekly service** (Lombard) [A/C].
> - They are also bid as **public works**: Illinois Prevailing Wage Act terms (Mokena 2026, Lincolnshire) and performance and payment bonds (Kane County Forest Preserve District) [A, DE-S017; C, DE-S018, DE-S019].
> - **Dozens of park districts and villages hold their own ILG87 pesticide-permit coverage**, so many can do the work in-house [A, DE-S025].
> - About 15 licensed private rivals compete, led by SOLitude (Rentokil).
>
> **The model.**
> - Base case: **year-3 operating profit $61k** and **year-5 ~$104k** (year 4 $126k).
> - Peak funding: **$68k before owner draws** ($120k with a $4k/month draw).
> - The ceiling is ~$250k a year unless the business is bundled with turf work (WS18-004) under the same IDOA licence and trucks.

## 1. Specific initial niche
Seasonal **algae and aquatic-weed programs for detention and retention ponds**: biweekly treatment, dye, readings and photo reports.

**Initial buyers:**
- HOAs and business-park owners through their property managers;
- smaller public bodies that already outsource: villages, park districts and forest preserves in **DuPage, Will, Kane and Lake counties**.

**Add-ons:** aeration fountain installs and seasonal service.

The founder holds the IDOA commercial applicator licence with the Aquatic category ($300 for 3 years) [A, WS13-S023].

- **Funnel:** 12 bids or quotes a month → 60% priced → 25% won (Wheaton took three quotes and awarded the low one [A]), about **1.8 new contracts a month**.
- **Unit economics:** CAC about $150. Lifetime value is about $350 × 85% / 1.5% ≈ $20k of gross profit.
- **Founder time:** 6–10 hours a week on bids and quotes in winter; in season the founder is the applicator.

## 7. Pricing strategy
Public pricing is visible and spread wide (3× between low and high bid at Wheaton).

**Price points:**
- **HOAs:** $1,200–$1,800 per pond per season for a biweekly algae program, inside the public $1,000–$2,900 band [A/C].
- **Weekly or premium:** $6,000–$8,000 per pond, with dissolved oxygen, pH and temperature reporting (Lombard) [A].

**Positioning:** never be the cheapest bidder. Win on documented reporting and response time where buyers select on qualifications (Addison's RFQ, D).

**Escalate price** for prevailing-wage public jobs.

## 9. Regulatory path
1. **IDOA commercial applicator licence.**
   - Pass the General Standards exam (100 questions, 70+) and the Aquatic category exam; online-proctored testing is available.
   - Fee $300 for 3 years; technicians need operator licences at $240.
   - Each facility needs a licensed applicator.
   - A certificate of insurance meeting 415 ILCS 60/10 is required [A, WS13-S023].
   - Timing: 1–3 months.
2. **NPDES pesticide general permit (ILG87).** About 15 private firms hold coverage [A, DE-S025]. NOI thresholds MISSING; confirm with IEPA.
3. **Public-works terms.**
   - Prevailing wages; IDOL schedules are effective for work on or after 2026-11-17, but rates are MISSING [A, DE-S038].
   - Employment of Illinois Workers on Public Works Act; certified payroll.
   - Bonds and additional-insured certificates [A, DE-S017].
4. **Not required:** lawn-care containment rules cover turf pesticides only [A, DE-S030]. They would apply if the business combines with WS18-004.
5. **Insurance:** general liability with pesticide and pollution coverage, auto, and inland marine for boats. About $650/month (E, no quotes).

## 10. Operations workflow
Bid or quote → contract → season plan per pond → biweekly route (truck plus jon boat, sprayer, dye, readings) → photo/reading report → monthly invoice → off-season fountain removal and storage, then bids.

**Human work:** application, boat handling, judging algae and weed species.

**AI-assisted:** bid drafting, report generation, routing and reminders. Software is Jobber Connect at $139/month.

AI does not change unit cost materially.

## 11. Competitive position and defensibility
**What accumulates:**
- route density (ponds per technician-day);
- multi-year public contracts with extensions (Kane FPD runs through 2028);
- HOA relationships through property managers;
- installed fountains (service revenue).

**Limits:** buyers rebid often, low bid wins, rivals are numerous, and SOLitude is an acquirer, which also makes it a possible exit.

**Bundling:** with turf (same IDOA licence, trucks and season) and stormwater-BMP inspection (WS11-021), the business could become a "licensed outdoor applicator" platform.

## 12. Risks and pre-mortem
| Loss level | How it happens |
|---|---|
| **$5k lost** | Licence, insurance and a few losing bids; no HOA traction because property managers stay with incumbents. |
| **$25k lost** | Truck and boat bought; 8–10 small contracts; a fish kill or misapplication complaint; prevailing-wage compliance costs eat the margin. |
| **$50k lost** | A technician hired on thin volume; DSO stretches to 60+ days; drought-year algae blooms bring re-treatment costs and lost renewals. |

**Early warnings:**
- win rate below 15% on 15+ bids;
- HOA price below $1,000 per pond;
- renewals below 80%;
- public buyers moving in-house (watch ILG87 filings).
