## 2. Customer discovery interview (non-leading, past behaviour) — DRAFT, not sent; requires founder review
**How to run it:** 30 minutes, ideally in person at the yard. Record answers verbatim. No pitching until question 18.

**About the company**
1. How many trucks and drivers do you run today? How many did you run 12 months ago?
2. Who in the company handles safety paperwork? Roughly how many hours a month does it take?

**Driver files and testing**
3. Walk me through the last time you hired a driver. What paperwork did you collect, and where is it kept?
4. When did you last run the annual Clearinghouse queries and MVR reviews? How did you do it?
5. Which testing consortium do you use? What did you pay last year, including per-test fees?

**Audits and enforcement**
6. Have you had a DOT audit, compliance review or roadside out-of-service order in the last 3 years? What did it cost
   you in time, fines or lost loads?
7. What compliance service or software have you paid for before? What did it cost, and why did you keep it or
   stop?

**Insurance**
8. When does your auto liability policy renew? How many quotes did you get last time, and from whom?
9. How did you choose your current agent? How long have you worked with them?
10. What did your premium do at the last renewal? What reason were you given?
11. In the last year, what did your agent do for you besides the renewal: certificates, filings, claims?
12. Have you changed agents or insurers in the last 3 years? What triggered it?

**Money and other services**
13. Who decides on spending like this, and who else needs to agree?
14. What do you pay for IFTA, IRP, UCR or permit services today, and to whom?
15. Do you use a factoring company, a dispatcher or an accountant? Who recommended them?
16. What is the most annoying recurring compliance task you deal with?

**Closing**
17. If the paperwork were handled for you, what would you do with the time?
18. *Only now:* may I show you what a file review would look like?

Afterwards, record the carrier's current insurer and filing month from FMCSA data next to the interview notes.

## 4. Sales call script with qualification — DRAFT, not sent; requires founder review
1. **Agenda (1 minute).** "I'll ask how you handle compliance today, show you what our review found, and if it fits
   we'll talk price. Fair?"
2. **Situation (5 minutes).** Use discovery questions 1–7 and 14.
3. **Review findings (10 minutes).** Go through the gap list: expired medical cards, missing annual reviews, no
   pre-employment query, random-pool status. Show the consequence of each with the regulation cited. Do not threaten
   fines.
4. **Qualification (BANT-style):**
   - Drivers: 3–20. Outside that range, refer elsewhere.
   - Registered 2020 or earlier.
   - The owner or office manager is the decision maker.
   - Current pain: an audit letter, upcoming renewal, a new hire, or a gap found.
   - Budget: pays today for consortium, permits or software ≥ $50/month.
   - Not in active bankruptcy and not out of service.
   - Insurance renewal month is known (or estimated from FMCSA data).
   - Willing to give us written authority to obtain loss runs and quote at renewal: yes / no / later.
5. **Offer.** Tier price, setup, consortium included, quarterly attestation. Plan is month-to-month after 3 months.
   Clients who place liability through us get 20% off.
6. **Insurance bridge.** "At T−90 we'll prepare your renewal package for you anyway. Would you like us to get a
   comparison quote through [host agency / our agency] at the same time?" If yes, collect signed authority and a
   loss-run request.
7. **Close.** E-sign the service agreement, ACH mandate and filing POA. Schedule onboarding within 5 business days.
8. **Disqualify politely** if they want software only (point to self-serve tools) or have 1–2 trucks (offer the
   consortium only).
