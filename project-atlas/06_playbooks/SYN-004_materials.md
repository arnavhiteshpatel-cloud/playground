## 2. Customer discovery questionnaire

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

Use this non-leading questionnaire for 30–40 minute conversations with a CFO, controller, owner or facilities head. Ask about past behaviour and actual spend. Do not pitch during the questions.

**Setting and authority**
1. Who in the company decided your current electricity supply contract? Who else signed off?
2. How many electric accounts or meters do you have in Illinois, and at which sites? Are any held by affiliated companies?

**Last purchase**
3. Walk me through how the last supply contract was chosen: when, how many offers, through whom.
4. What product did you choose (fixed, index, blocks)? Why?
5. When does it expire? Is there an early-termination clause?
6. Was a broker or consultant involved? Do you know how they were paid, and how much?

**Capacity and demand**
7. Does your bill show a capacity or "PLC" charge, or is it bundled into the price?
8. Has anyone ever shown you your capacity tag or the summer peak hours that set it?
9. Did you do anything during last summer's peak days? What did it cost you operationally?
10. Have you ever enrolled in demand response?
    - If yes: with whom, how many kW, what were you paid last year, and how much did the provider keep?
    - If no: has anyone offered it? Why did you decline?

**Refrigeration operations**
11. How much can you pre-cool or shift compressors and defrost cycles, and for how long, without product risk?
12. Do you have backup generators? Are they permitted for non-emergency runs?

**Spend, pain and fit**
13. Roughly what did electricity cost last year in total? Has it changed materially?
14. Who else has approached you about energy in the last 12 months? What happened?
15. If we reviewed 12 months of bills, what would you want to learn? Who would need to see the result?

**Close and record**
- Close: "Would you be willing to share 12 months of bills, or sign a ComEd interval-data authorisation, for a free review?" Record yes or no.
- Record in CRM: aggregate kW estimate, expiry date, current broker, DR history, decision maker, next step.

## 4. Sales call script with qualification

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

1. **Agenda (1 min).** "Three things: how you buy power today, what the review shows, and whether a next step makes sense."
2. **Qualification (10 min). Stop supply discussion if Q1 fails.**
   - **Q1. Exemption:** "How many electric accounts does the company and its affiliates have in Illinois? What's the highest monthly demand on each last year?" Sum >1,500 kW required for any supply conversation; confirm from bills.
   - **Q2. Timing:** "When does the current supply contract end?" Within 18 months scores high; beyond that, offer PLC/DR now and the RFP later.
   - **Q3. Capacity exposure:** "Is capacity passed through on your bill or fixed in the price?"
   - **Q4. Flexibility:** "Can you shift compressor load for 1–4 hours on a hot afternoon?"
   - **Q5. Decision:** "Who signs, and what would they need to see?"
3. **Findings (10 min).** Capacity tag and its cost at $325 and at $337.83/MW-day; contract expiry; DR estimate in kW with a range; any billing anomalies.
4. **Offer (5 min).**
   - (a) Run an RFP among 4–6 suppliers at the right time, for a disclosed fee of [0.75–1.25] mils.
   - (b) Summer PLC alerts.
   - (c) A competitive DR bid among CSPs.
   - Show the customer the fee in dollars per year.
5. **Objections.**
   - **"We already have a broker."** "Fine. When does the agreement end, and do you know the fee? We disclose ours in writing and show all bids."
   - **"Brokers take hidden kickbacks."** "Our fee is in the agency agreement you sign and in the supplier's confirmation."
   - **"We tried DR and got penalised."** Ask what happened; review performance before re-enrolling.
6. **Next step.** Signed agency agreement plus data authorisation, or a dated follow-up tied to the expiry.

## 7. Pricing calculator spec

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

**Inputs, per account:**
- annual kWh;
- monthly billed kW per account, and the list of affiliated accounts;
- current contract price and expiry;
- capacity tag (kW) and transmission tag;
- capacity price by delivery year: $325 (2028/29), $337.83 cap / $181.91 floor (2029/30) [A, E-SYN004-S009], plus a user value for later years;
- product type (fixed or pass-through);
- curtailable kW (ICAP) and DR accreditation factor (default 0.91 [A, E-SYN004-S010]);
- customer DR share offered by each CSP;
- partner share or advisory fee %;
- PLC reduction kW and PLC fee %;
- broker fee in mils;
- supplier payment terms;
- licensed-partner split (if ≤1,500 kW).

**Outputs:**
- exemption flag (sum of max billed kW >1,500);
- annual broker fee in $ and as % of supply cost;
- PLC savings $/yr and fee;
- DR gross $/yr (kW × factor × price × 365), customer share, our share;
- overlap check: DR kW plus PLC-reduction kW must not exceed PLC minus firm service level [D, E-SYN004-S014];
- first cash date (contract start + ~45–60 days);
- 3-year contribution after splits and a 3% true-up allowance;
- customer net benefit.

**Rules:**
- Block supply pricing output when the exemption flag is false and no licence or partner is selected.
- Show fees in dollars per year for disclosure.

## 8. Order-intake / onboarding checklist

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

- [ ] Exemption file complete: accounts list, affiliate list, 12 bills each, computed aggregate billed demand >1,500 kW, reviewer initials.
- [ ] Signed agency/representation agreement, with the fee in mils and in $/yr, term, termination, and the confidentiality and data-use clause.
- [ ] ComEd interval-data authorisation (LOA) signed.
- [ ] Current contract terms captured: expiry, early-termination fee, swing.
- [ ] Supplier broker agreement active for each bidder; customer registered with each supplier as our client (avoids broker conflicts [B, E-SYN004-S007]).
- [ ] RFP issued; bids logged; all bids shown to the customer; usage estimate identical across bids.
- [ ] Customer signs the supplier contract. Fee confirmation letter received and checked against the agency agreement.
- [ ] CRM: expiry date, renewal reminder at minus 12 and minus 9 months, expected monthly fee.
- [ ] PLC: summer alert contacts and site playbook. DR: CSP bid, chosen CSP agreement, nomination, test date.
- [ ] Remittance reconciliation set up (expected kWh × mils against received; variance >10% flagged).
- [ ] Records retained for 3 years.
