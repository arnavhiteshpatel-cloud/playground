## 2. Customer discovery interview questionnaire (non-leading; past behaviour and spend)

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.** 30 minutes. Do not pitch until question 20. Record answers verbatim.

**Context**
1. What kind of work does your company do? Roughly what was revenue last year?
2. What share of last year's work was public: City, state, school district or federal?
3. How many jobs are open right now? How many are prevailing-wage?

**Bonding (past behaviour)**
4. When did you last apply for a bond or a bond-line increase? What did the surety ask you to provide?
5. Who prepared those documents? How long did it take?
6. In the last 12 months, did a surety, bank or GC ask for a WIP schedule or interim statements? What did you send?
7. Has a bond, bid or line increase ever been delayed or declined because of financial paperwork? What happened?

**Books**
8. Who keeps your books today: an in-house person, an outside bookkeeper or your CPA? Cash or accrual?
9. What do you pay for that per month, including that person's time if in-house?
10. What did your CPA bill last year for year-end work? Was any of it clean-up?
11. Can you see profit by job today? When did you last check a job's margin before it finished?

**Certified payroll**
12. Who prepares your Illinois certified payrolls? How many hours does a typical month take?
13. In the last year, has a GC or owner held a payment because a certified payroll was missing or wrong?
14. Do you also report on owner systems (PBC LCPtracker, B2GNow, City compliance)? How long does that take?
15. What payroll system do you use? Can it export hours by job and classification?

**Decision and switching**
16. When you last changed a bookkeeper or payroll provider, what triggered it? Who decided?
17. What would have to be true for you to change how this is done?
18. Who else do you rely on for this: your surety agent, CPA or a GC?

**Close**
19. Is there anything I should have asked?
20. *(Only now)* "We are offering a $450 WIP and surety-readiness check. Would that be useful, or not?" Record the exact answer.

## 4. Sales call script with qualification questions

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

1. **Agenda (1 min):** "I'd like to understand your situation. If it fits, I'll explain what we do and what it costs. If not, I'll say so."
2. **Qualification** (disqualify if two or more are "no"):
   - Revenue $1–15M, and at least one job above ~$500k or a bonded backlog above ~$1M? *(WIP fit)*
   - Active or expected prevailing-wage work in the next 6 months? *(certified-payroll fit)*
   - QuickBooks Online or Desktop, and willing to grant read-only access? *(delivery fit)*
   - Payroll on a platform that exports hours by job/class (QB Payroll, ADP, Gusto, Paychex)? *(data fit)*
   - Decision-maker on the call (owner, or owner plus office manager)?
   - Current monthly spend on books/payroll admin of at least ~$700, including staff time? *(budget fit)*
3. **Pain confirmation:** "What happened the last time the surety or a GC asked for something you didn't have?"
4. **Offer:** recommend the Check ($450) or the right tier (Core $750, Surety-ready $1,100, Public-works $1,400 + $125 per extra project). Always state:
   - "You approve worker classifications";
   - "You certify each payroll";
   - "We don't give legal opinions";
   - "Your CPA still does your year-end and any review."
5. **Objections:**
   - *"My CPA does this."* → "How often? Is it monthly, or just year-end?"
   - *"Too expensive."* → "Certified-payroll software alone runs $145–667 a month before anyone's time." [B, E-SYN006-S010/S013]
   - *"We do it in-house."* → "How many hours a month? What happens when that person is out?"
6. **Close:** engagement letter, read-only access request, ACH authorization, start date.

## 7. Pricing calculator spec

**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

**Inputs:**
- revenue band;
- number of bank and credit accounts;
- monthly transactions;
- open jobs;
- jobs above $500k;
- WIP frequency (quarterly or semiannual);
- active prevailing-wage projects by month;
- federal (WH-347) projects;
- owner-system reports (PBC/B2GNow/Illinois Works);
- months of clean-up needed;
- payroll employees;
- payroll platform (exportable Y/N);
- annual prepay (Y/N).

**Hours model (E; recalibrate with time tracking):**
- bookkeeper hours = 3 + 0.01 × transactions + 0.5 × open jobs;
- senior review = 0.75 + 0.25 × jobs above $500k;
- compliance = 1.5 h per active IDOL project-month (+1 h for each owner system);
- clean-up = 3 h per month rebuilt.

**Outputs:**
- recommended tier;
- monthly price (tier + $125 per extra project + owner-system add-ons);
- one-time clean-up price;
- estimated labour cost at loaded rates ($34/$55/$33 per hour);
- **contribution and contribution %**;
- warning flags.

**Flags:**
- contribution below 45%;
- expected hours above 12 a month;
- non-exportable payroll;
- any job without PM estimates.

**Rule:** never quote below a 45% contribution. Re-quote whenever open jobs change by ±30%.
