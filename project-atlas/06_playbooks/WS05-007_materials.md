## B. Customer discovery interview (research only; non-leading; past behaviour and spend) (DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review)

**Opener (in person, at a quiet time, 10 minutes):**
"I'm researching how Chicago restaurants and food shops handle pest control. I'm not selling anything today. Could I ask you eight quick questions? Your answers stay anonymous."

1. Who handles pest control here now: an outside company, yourselves, or nobody at the moment?
2. How long have you used your current company? How did you find them?
3. How often do they visit (monthly, every other month, twice a year, on call)?
4. Roughly what do you pay per visit or per month? Was there a setup or initial charge? (Accept a range; do not suggest numbers.)
5. Is there a written contract? How long is it, and how much notice is needed to cancel?
6. In the past 12 months, have you changed pest companies, or seriously looked at changing? What happened?
7. Think about the last time pests caused you a problem (an inspection, a complaint, a sighting). What did you do, how quickly did your company respond, and what did it cost?
8. Who decides on a vendor like this (owner, manager, head office)? What would they need to see before changing?

**Close:** "Thank you. Would it be all right if I came back after my business is licensed, to offer a free inspection?" Record yes or no. **Offer nothing now.**

**Tally sheet fields:**

- Has a vendor (Y/N)
- Vendor name, if volunteered

- Switched or considered switching in the past 12 months (Y/N)

**E1 decision rule** (playbook Section 4):

## D. Sales call script and qualification (on-site, post-licence) (DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review)

1. **Permission and agenda (1 min).** "I'll look at the kitchen, storage, receiving door and trash area, then tell you what I see. No obligation."
2. **Inspection (10 min).**
   - Photograph activity and entry points.
   - Note door gaps, sweeps and drains.
   - Check for a current logbook.
3. **Qualification questions:**
   - "Who decides on pest service, and is anyone else involved?" (decision-maker)
   - "Do you have a current contract? When can it be cancelled, and with how much notice?" (timing)
   - "What do you pay now, roughly?" (budget anchor; don't lead)
   - "What would a good pest company do differently for you?" (pain)
   - "Have you had a citation or customer complaint about pests in the last year?" (urgency; they may already know it is public)
   - "Do you also run another location?" (expansion)
4. **Recommendation (3 min).**
   - Initial cleanout and exclusion: [$150–300].
   - Monthly service: [$125] (high-volume: twice monthly at [$200–250]).
   - 12-month agreement, 30-day cancellation, 3% annual adjustment.
   - What they get every visit: report, logbook copy, and 24-hour response to sightings.
5. **Objections:**
   - *"We already have someone."* "Many owners keep their company. If you change, I start with exclusion so the problem doesn't return. What's your notice period?"
   - *"Too expensive."* Offer the twice-monthly-to-monthly step-down, or quarterly for low-risk sites. **Never go below the $95-per-visit floor.**
   - *"Need to think."* Book a specific follow-up date and leave the written report.
6. **Close.**
   - E-signature on the tablet, card on file or net-15, first service date.
   - Hand over the certificate of insurance if asked.
7. **Disqualify** if:
   - the site is outside the five ZIPs (refer out);
   - the buyer wants twice-a-year service below $95 per visit;
   - the work is termite or fumigation;
   - the site is a chain decided at head office (log for later).

## G. Pricing calculator specification (DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review)

**Inputs:**
- Facility type (restaurant / high-volume kitchen / grocer / bakery / wholesale plant / supportive living / residential 2–6 flat).
- Floor area (sq ft); number of exterior doors; basement (Y/N).
- Pest pressure (none, light, active, repeat-cited); number of public citations in 12 months.
- Visit frequency (twice monthly, monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly).
- Number of devices (interior and exterior stations, monitors, insect-light traps).
- Drive minutes from the route centroid.
- Audit requirement (Y/N; scheme).
- Materials cost per visit, from the supplier price list.
- Loaded labour rate ($28/h default).
- Target contribution margin (default ≥55% after labour and vehicle).

**Calculations:**
- Visit minutes = base minutes by type + 2 min × devices + 15 min if audit reporting.
- Labour cost per visit = (visit minutes + drive minutes) / 60 × labour rate.
- Vehicle cost per visit = drive minutes × $[0.60]/min.
- Price per visit = (materials + labour + vehicle) / (1 − target margin − 3% fees).
- Monthly price = price per visit × visits per month, then rounded up to the price list tier.
- Floor: never below $95 per visit or $100/month.
- Initial cleanout = devices installed × device cost × 1.8 + exclusion hours × $[85]/h.

**Outputs:**
- Quoted monthly price and initial price.
- Contribution per month and margin %.
- Annual contract value.
- Payback of device cost (months).
- Flag if margin is below 45%, or the site is outside the five ZIPs.

## J. Prospect data-pull specification (free Chicago open data; no API key; no AI needed) (DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review)

**Source:** `https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/4ijn-s7e5.json` (Food Inspections; updated daily).

**Query** (SoQL, last 30 days, cluster ZIPs):
- `$select` = `license_,dba_name,address,zip,facility_type,risk,inspection_date,inspection_type,results,violations`
- `$where` = `inspection_date >= '[today−30]' AND zip in ('60608','60609','60616','60623','60632')`
- `$limit` = `5000`

**Parse:** take the violation segment starting "38. INSECTS, RODENTS"; use the text after "Comments:".

**Classify:**
- **Pest activity** if the comment matches: DROPPING | EXCRETA | MICE | MOUSE | RAT(S) | ROACH | LIVE | FLIES | GNAT | FRUIT FL | INSECT ACTIVITY | (FLYING|CRAWLING|DEAD) INSECTS | NUMEROUS INSECTS | \d+ INSECTS | BED BUG.
  - Strip "RODENT PROOF" first.
  - Gap-only comments ("OPENING", "GAP", "DOOR SWEEP") are the **exclusion** class.
  - Logbook comments are the **logbook** class.

**Rank:**
1. Pest activity in ≥2 inspections in 12 months (repeat).
2. Failed re-inspection.
3. Pest activity with a pass at re-inspection.
4. New "License" inspection.
5. Exclusion or logbook only.

Exclude: "Out of Business", schools, national chains.

**Output:** a route-ordered walk list for each corridor (26th St; Cermak/Archer; Wentworth/Chinatown; Ashland/Back of the Yards; Kedzie/Pulaski), with the date of the latest record and the earliest walk-in date (inspection date + 7 days).

**Companion queries:**
- New Retail Food licences: `r5kz-chrr`, `application_type='ISSUE'`.
- Residential rodent signal: 311 dataset `v6vf-nfxy`, `sr_type='Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint'`, grouped by ZIP.

Use the data only to choose which businesses to visit in person. Do not scrape personal data, and do not contact inspectors.
