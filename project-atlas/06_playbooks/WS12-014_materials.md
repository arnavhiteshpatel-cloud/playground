# WS12-014 — Draft Materials: Controls & Automation Engineering Recruiting Desk (Illinois)

> **DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.** Nothing here has been sent, submitted or used. Square brackets mark placeholders.

**Compliance rules for every message and script** (see playbook §5.4):
- **Never ask a candidate's current or past salary.** Ask what pay they *expect*. Illinois bars employment agencies from screening on, or requiring, salary history [A, E-WS12014-S003].
- **Never charge a candidate anything**, and never offer résumé or coaching services for a fee. Doing so would void the licensing exclusion [A, E-WS12014-S001].
- **Pay bands:**
  - Include the employer's pay scale and benefits (or a link to them) in any posting made for an employer with 15+ employees.
  - Give the band to any candidate who asks [A, E-WS12014-S003].
- **No criminal-history questions** before interview selection [A, E-WS12014-S004].
- **AI:**
  - No AI-analysed video interviews [A, E-WS12014-S005].
  - AI is used only for drafting and notes, never for ranking or rejecting people [C, E-WS12014-S007].
- **Consent before submittal.** Present a candidate to an employer only with that candidate's written consent for that specific role.
- **Outreach:** emails identify the sender and include an opt-out. Calls are manual only: no autodialers or bulk texts (E; confirm with counsel).

## 2. Customer discovery interview (hiring managers / HR), non-leading, past behaviour
> DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review. 20 minutes. Do not pitch; listen. Record answers in the CRM.

**Context**
1. What does your team build or maintain? Which control platforms: Rockwell, Siemens, Emerson, Honeywell, other?
2. How many controls, automation or I&C people do you have, and how many have you hired in the last 24 months?

**Last hire (past behaviour)**
3. Walk me through the last controls hire you made. When did the role open, and when did the person start?
4. Where did the hire come from: referral, job board, internal recruiter, agency, other?
5. What did that hire cost you? Include agency fees, ads and time.
6. What went wrong or took longest?

**Agency use**
7. In the last two years, have you paid an outside recruiter for any engineering role? What fee percentage, and what guarantee?
8. How did that agency get in the door?
9. Is there a vendor-approval process, or a portal, for new recruiting firms? Who owns it?
10. What made you stop using an agency, if you did?

**Current role**
11. The posting for [role] went up on [date]. What has happened since?
12. What does the ideal candidate have that the applicants so far did not?
13. What is the approved pay band? Is there flexibility for the right person?
14. If a strong candidate appeared this week, what would the interview steps be and how long would they take?

**Close (only if appropriate)**
15. Would you look at one anonymised profile if I have someone who matches?

## 4. Sales call script with qualification questions
> DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review. Goal: a signed fee agreement for a specific role, or a clear "no".

1. **Confirm the role is real and open.** "Is the [Role] still open? Approved headcount and budget?" Disqualify if it is frozen or filled.
2. **Pain.** "How long has it been open? What has that delayed?"
3. **Decision process:**
   - "Who decides on the hire?"
   - "Who approves an outside recruiter? Do we need vendor approval or a portal?"
   - If it is portal-only, note the owner and the steps. Do not proceed without an agreement.
4. **Profile.**
   - "Must-haves vs nice-to-haves? Platforms? Travel percentage? On-site?"
   - "What is the approved pay band and the benefits summary?" These are needed for disclosure.
5. **Competition.** "Are other agencies or your internal team working it? How many candidates are in process?"
6. **Timeline.** "Interview steps and turnaround? Who can I schedule with directly?"
7. **Terms:**
   - "My fee is 20% of first-year base, invoiced on start, net-30."
   - "There is a 90-day replacement guarantee, and candidates I introduce are mine for 12 months."
   - "For a first role I can do 18%, or 18% across two roles if you give me two weeks' exclusivity."
8. **Commitment.** "If I send the agreement today and you sign it, I'll send two vetted profiles within [5] business days."

**Qualification scorecard** (proceed if ≥4 of 6):
- open ≥30 days;
- band ≥ $90k;
- hiring manager engaged;
- no portal block;
- ≤2 other agencies;
- interview steps ≤3 weeks.

## 7. Pricing calculator spec (inputs → outputs)
> DRAFT — specification for a spreadsheet; requires founder review.

**Inputs:**
- Candidate first-year base salary (S).
- Fee percentage (f): default 20%, floor 18%.
- Split share (s): 0 if not a split; 0.5 for a 50/50 split.
- Fallout/discount reserve (r): default 15%.
- Direct search costs (ads, InMail overage, travel) (c).
- Expected days to start (t).
- Client DSO (d): default 40.
- Hours of desk time (h).
- Founder hourly opportunity value (v).

**Outputs:**

| Output | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross fee | F = S × f |
| Retained fee | F × (1 − s) |
| Expected realized fee | F × (1 − s) × (1 − r) |
| Contribution | Expected realized fee − c |
| Contribution per desk hour | Contribution ÷ h |
| Cash date | Today + t + d |
| Guarantee expiry | Start + 90 days |
| Floor check | Flags if f < 18%, S < $90k, or contribution per hour < v |

**Comparison to model (playbook §3):**
- Base transaction: S = $105k, f = 20%, s = 0, r = 15%, c = $420 → contribution $17,430.
- Split technician: S = $72k, f = 18%, s = 0.5 → $5,358.

## 10. Technical-screen template (founder's wedge)
> DRAFT — requires founder review; validate the questions with a practising controls engineer before use.

| Area | What to cover |
|---|---|
| Platform depth | Which PLC families, versions and HMI/SCADA packages have you shipped to production? Walk me through one project from FAT to SAT |
| Scenario 1 (logic) | A conveyor jams intermittently after a sensor upgrade. How do you diagnose it? *Listen for:* scan time, debounce, I/O mapping, trending |
| Scenario 2 (alarms) | Operators ignore alarms on a line. What do you change? *Listen for:* alarm rationalisation, priorities, ISA-18.2 awareness |
| Scenario 3 (networks / safety) | Adding a safety PLC or remote I/O on an existing network. What do you check? |
| Commissioning | Hardest start-up you supported: what broke, what you did, how long on site |
| Documentation and standards | Version control, P&IDs, ISA-88/95 if batch or MES |
| Motivation and constraints | Travel tolerance, on-site needs, pay *expectations*, timing, counter-offer risk |

**Scoring:**
- Each area scored 1–5.
- Submit only if the average is ≥3.5 and no area scores 1.
- Attach a 5-line written summary to the submittal.
