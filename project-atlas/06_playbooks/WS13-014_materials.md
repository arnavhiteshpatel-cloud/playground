## 2. Customer discovery interview (E1). Past behaviour only, non-leading
**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

**Target:** a project manager, foreman or superintendent at an electrical, plumbing, HVAC or interiors firm on the permit list. 12–15 minutes. Do not pitch until the last question.

**Opening:** "I'm researching how contractors handle scanning before coring and cutting. Could I ask about the last time you needed it? I'm not selling anything in this conversation."

**Past-behaviour questions:**
1. When did your crew last need a slab or wall scanned before coring or cutting? What was the job (building type; PT or not)?
2. Who did the scan: your own people, the coring sub, a scanning firm or the GC's vendor? How was that vendor chosen?
3. Roughly what did that scan cost: per half-day, per day or per hole? Was it billed to you or to the GC? (If unsure: "Could you check the last invoice?")
4. How many days ahead did you have to book? Has a scan ever delayed a core date? What happened?
5. In the last 12 months, about how many times did your company order scanning in the Chicago area?
6. What did you receive: marks only, a PDF, photos? What did you do with it?
7. Has anything ever gone wrong (a missed conduit, a re-scan, a no-show)? What did it cost?
8. Outdoors: when you dig on private property, who locates the private lines? What did the last one cost?
9. What do you need from a scanning vendor before the first job (COI wording, prequal platform, safety documents)?
10. Who else in your company books these: the PM, the foreman or the office?

**Only at the end (the commitment test):**
- "I'm opening a service at $595 a half-day, with a $550 first visit, next-business-day booking and the report the same day. Is there a dated core or cut in the next 30 days you'd book me for once I send our COI?"
- Record: yes with a date / maybe / no, and why.

**Scoring sheet (per interview):**
- last price, and whether it was quoted or invoiced;
- wait in days;
- vendor type;
- frequency per year;
- pain (0–2);
- prequalification needs;
- commitment (date / maybe / no).

## 4. Sales call script with qualification
**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

1. **Context:** "What's the job, and how many penetrations? PT slab or not? Occupied building?"
2. **Current solution:** "Who scans for you now? What do you like or dislike about it?"
3. **Timing:** "When is the first core date? How often do you core on this job?"
4. **Decision:** "Who approves a new scanning vendor: you, the PM or the GC? What do they need (COI wording, prequal, safety program)?"
5. **Price check:** "Our half-day is $595 for up to 4 hours on site, and the first visit is $550. Does that fit how you buy this?" (Record their stated last price.)
6. **Close for a date:** "Can I pencil in [date] for [floor]? I'll send the COI and confirmation today."

**Qualification (all four needed for a priced first job):**
- a dated job within 30 days;
- the decision-maker identified;
- the insurance form is one we can meet;
- price accepted at ≥ $550.

**Disqualify or defer if:**
- the GC mandates a national vendor;
- the only need is free scanning bundled by the coring sub;
- the slab is unscannable with GPR (refer to X-ray).

- mileage rate ($0.725 for Jan–Jun 2026 or $0.76 for Jul–Dec 2026 [A, E-WS13014-S016]);
- kit depreciation per visit (default $15 slab, $30 locate);
- consumables (default $10–20);
- payment method (card at 3% fee, or ACH/net-30).

**Rules:**
- base price comes from the rate card;
- the rate card snaps to half-day or full-day blocks;
- add the after-hours and outer-zone adders;
- **never quote a half-day below $550**;
- flag any quote where contribution after technician time is below $250, or below 40%.

**Outputs:**
- quoted price;
- direct cost breakdown;
- contribution in $ and %;
- for rented kit, the break-even number of jobs per rental week;
- expected cash date (invoice date + DSO);
- a warning if PT, after-hours or outer-zone applies and was not priced.

## 9. Weekly KPI sheet spec

| KPI | Definition | Gate or alarm |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified conversations | PM/foreman conversations with a dated need discussed | Base plan 22 a month at full pace (E) |

| Paid visits | Completed and invoiced | Pilot: ≥ 10 in 60 days |

| Repeat accounts | Accounts with ≥ 2 paid visits | **≥ 3 within 60 days of first job** |
| Active accounts | Paid visit in the last 60 days | Month 6 ≥ 6; month 9 ≥ 14; hire gate ≥ 20 |
| ARPC (monthly) | Revenue ÷ active accounts | Hire gate ≥ $650 |
| Billable visits per field person | Half-day equivalents a month | Founder ≥ 22 for 2 months before hiring; technician ≥ 18 |
| Backlog | Business days to the next open slot | > 5 days = capacity signal |
| Lost-quote reasons | Price / timing / incumbent / prequal | Price > 40% of losses = pricing alarm |
| Churn | Accounts lapsing ÷ active | Alarm > 5% a month |
| DSO | Receivables ÷ revenue × 30 | Alarm > 50 days |
| Quality | Re-scans, complaints, mis-marks, strikes | **Any mis-mark → root cause within 48 h; any strike → stop and review** |
| Cash | Cumulative owner capital deployed | Alarm if > $50k; hard cap ~$60k |
| Founder hours | Field / sales / admin | Alarm > 55 h/week for 3 weeks |

**Weekly review:**
- 30 minutes every Friday;
- update the 13-week cash forecast;

## 10. Scan report template (outline)
**DRAFT — requires founder review.**

1. Header: customer, GC, address, floor or area, date, technician, equipment model and serial, calibration date.
2. Scope as requested; areas actually scanned (marked-up sketch or photo).
3. Results table: location ID, target type (rebar, PT tendon suspected, conduit, unknown), approximate depth, confidence (high/medium/low), recommendation (clear, shift, no-cut).
4. Photographs of every mark with a scale.
5. Limitations statement (as in the quote).
6. Not a survey; not an ASCE 38 quality-level deliverable.
7. Foreman acknowledgement (name, signature or text confirmation).
