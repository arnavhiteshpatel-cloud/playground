## B. Customer discovery interview questionnaire
**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

- **Purpose:** learn past behaviour and spend. Do not pitch.
- **Format:** about 20 minutes; note-taking only; ask permission to record figures.
- **Rule:** ask what they *did*, not what they *would* do.

1. **Role context**
   - What is your role?
   - Who in the company decides on branded apparel, safety gear and recognition items?
   - Is it the same budget?
2. **Last order**
   - Think of the last time you ordered branded shirts, hoodies or hats for crews. Roughly when was it?
   - What items, about how many, and from whom?
3. **Price paid** (the key question for V1)
   - Do you remember the unit price, or could you look at the invoice for those items?
   - Was there a setup or digitizing charge?
4. **Second-to-last order:** same questions (items, quantities, vendor, unit prices).
5. **What went wrong**
   - On those orders, did anything go wrong: late, wrong sizes, logo issues, missing pieces?
   - What did that cost you in time?
6. **New hires**
   - When someone new starts on a crew, what do they get on day one?
   - Who hands it out?
   - Where is it stored?
7. **Annual pattern**
   - Over the last 12 months, about how many times did you order branded items?
   - Are any of them tied to a season or event (Safety Week, holidays, winter gear)?
8. **Hi-vis**
   - Which ANSI class do your sites or GCs require?
   - Do you buy hi-vis with your logo, or plain?
   - Who supplies it?
9. **Union**
   - Does anyone ask for union-made or union-printed items?
   - Has it ever decided a purchase?
10. **Recognition**
    - Do you run any safety recognition or awards?
    - What did you give last time?
    - Gift cards or items?
11. **Payment:** how did you pay the last vendor (card, check, net 30)? Do new vendors have to go through an approval or onboarding process?
12. **Alternatives:** have you ever ordered from an online vendor such as 4imprint or Custom Ink? What happened?
13. **Spend estimate:** roughly what did the company spend on branded apparel and gear last year? A range is fine.
14. **Close:** is there anyone else (a GC partner, another sub) I should talk to about how they handle this?

Do not ask "Would you buy from us?" or "Is this a good idea?"

**Record:** vendor; items; quantity; unit price; date; pains; decision-maker; terms; ANSI and union needs; spend range.

## D. Sales call script with qualification questions
**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.** Use only after the discovery stage (V3 onward).

1. **Agenda** (30 s): "I'll ask about your crews and upcoming needs, show what we do, and if it fits, quote one order."
2. **Qualification** (score 1 point for each):
   - ≥20 field employees;
   - has ordered branded gear in the past 12 months;
   - an identified decision-maker on the call;
   - a dated need within 60 days (hires, Safety Week, season);
   - will pay a 50% deposit or by card;
   - not locked into a rental contract for the same items.
   **Proceed if ≥4 points.**
3. **Discovery:**
   - crew counts by trade;
   - size distribution (or the last roster);
   - logo files;
   - ANSI class;
   - union-label requirement;
   - deadline;
   - delivery points.
4. **Present:** sample kit, the yard-delivery and size-run promise, the reorder process, Safety Week package. Optionally the recognition program: the leading-indicator design and tangible awards. Say clearly: "your tax adviser confirms treatment; we don't give tax advice."
5. **Price conversation:**
   - Quote per item, with setup shown separately.
   - If they say "4imprint is cheaper", reply: "On some items yes. Here's what's included: sorting by person, yard delivery, reorders from your size file, replacement of our errors at our cost."
   - Never go below the pricing-calculator floor.
6. **Close:** "Shall I send the proof and the deposit invoice today so we hold the [DATE] delivery?"
7. **After the call:** log it in the CRM; send the quote within 24 hours; set follow-ups at 3 days and 7 days.

## G. Pricing calculator specification
**DRAFT — not sent; requires founder review.**

**Purpose:** every quote line hits a gross-margin floor and is benchmarked against 4imprint's published price.

**Inputs per line:**

| Input | Notes |
|---|---|
| Item / style; supplier | — |
| Quantity; size mix | % of 2XL+; drives blank upcharges |
| Blank net cost by size | From SanMar/S&S account; fallback to public price, flagged |
| Decoration method | screen / embroidery / DTF / laser |
| Locations; colours or stitch count | — |
| Decoration cost per piece at that quantity | From the decorator's trade list |
| Setup / screen / digitizing / tape cost | — |
| Inbound freight per piece; outbound / delivery cost | — |
| Card fee % and share paid by card | — |
| Reprint reserve % | Default 1.5% |
| 4imprint published price at the nearest quantity tier | Manual entry, with date |
| Supplier list price and discount code | If supplier-decorated |
| Target GM % | Default 32% |
| Floor GM % | 30% blended; 25% stores; 20% absolute line floor |

**Outputs per line:**
- landed unit cost;
- price at target GM;
- price at floor GM;
- **price index vs 4imprint** (= our price ÷ 4imprint price);
- price index vs supplier list;
- line GM % and GM $;
- a red flag if the index is below 1.00 and GM is below the floor, or if any cost is a public-price fallback.

**Order outputs:**
- blended GM %;
- contribution after card fees and reserve;
- deposit amount;
- cash-gap days (supplier payment date vs expected customer payment);
- tariff-sensitivity line (GM if blank cost +10%).

**Post-order outputs:** realized GM from actual supplier invoices; variance vs quote. These feed the KPI sheet.
