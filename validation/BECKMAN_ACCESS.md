**Staleness warning:** the page states rates were set by University Government Costing, **effective August 1, 2021, "subject to change"** **[V]**. That is more than five years ago. The rates may have been revised without the page changing, or the page may simply be out of date **[INF]**. **Confirm the current rate in writing before budgeting.** The 2026 date in the footer is only the site copyright **[V]**.

## 4. Access procedure (from the [Access page](https://microscopy.beckman.illinois.edu/about/access)) [V]

1. Contact Suite staff to schedule an **orientation** and set up an account.
2. Complete the **registration form** (contact and billing info) before training.
3. Email itg-ms@beckman.illinois.edu with: NetID, **CFOP**, department, group, advisor (if applicable), email, campus address, phone, and a brief project description.
4. **Non-Urbana users** must submit a facilities usage agreement.
5. You need an Urbana **i-card**; a temporary card is possible.
6. **Training on each instrument** is by appointment. Length depends on technique complexity and experience.
7. After orientation, activate door access at **Beckman Security (room 1237)**.
8. Once registered, users have **24/7 access**.

## 5. Policies relevant to this experiment

From the [Policies page](https://microscopy.beckman.illinois.edu/about/policies) **[V]**:
- Fees apply to initial training, access and assisted use.
- Scheduling: users should not block large periods over multiple days. The cap is **8 hours blocked at a single time per user**, and multi-day blocks need prior discussion with staff.
- No sharing of i-cards, keys or accounts.
- Equipment stays in the Suite.
- The access form asks users to agree to **Chem Safety, Laser Safety and X-ray Safety** policies.
- All UIUC policies apply.
- **The page has no specific policy on biological tissue, fixatives or containment [UNR].** Ask staff.

From [Data Storage](https://microscopy.beckman.illinois.edu/about/data-storage) **[V]**: Suite computers mount the user's Home drive (Z:), **CTData (W:)** and Groups (P:) on `files.itg.beckman.illinois.edu`. Off-campus access is via VPN.
- **[INF]** By default, data lands on University servers. Ask OTM whether that matters (Q A7), and export immediately to founder-owned drives with SHA-256 checksums.

University safety training: Division of Research Safety pages state that users of radiation-producing machines must complete DRS online X-Ray Safety training at intervals **not exceeding 12 months**, plus equipment-specific training from the registrant **[RF]** (search summary of [DRS authorization page](https://drs.illinois.edu/Programs/RegisteringRadiationProducingMachines/)). Confirm with Suite staff which course is required for Versa users.

## 6. Questions to send the Suite (also in draft email 1)

1. Which billing category applies to an independent, not-yet-incorporated student project? Is a CFOP or a contract required?
2. Are the 2021-08-01 rates current? How many training hours are typical for the Versa 630 for a user new to micro-CT?
3. Is **assisted** (staff-run) scanning available, and at what rate? The page charges for "assisted use" but lists no separate rate [UNR].
4. Rules for animal tissue: is the BL-2 room (B420) required? Which containment is acceptable in the scanner (sealed tube, double containment)? Ethanol versus formalin?
5. Is a hydroxyapatite (HA) **density calibration phantom** available to users?
6. Is a Versa **autoloader or batch scanning** available for overnight multi-sample runs [UNR]? How are unattended overnight hours billed?
7. Which reconstruction options are standard (ZEISS reconstruction software versions, beam-hardening correction)? Can users disable any AI/deep-learning reconstruction for ground-truth experiments [INF: ZEISS offers DL reconstruction options on Versa systems; confirm what is installed]?
8. Maximum sample diameter and height and holder types. Do 50 mL conical tubes fit the standard mount?
9. Can raw projections and reconstructions be exported (.txrm/.txm and TIFF), and in what bit depth?
10. Data retention period on CTData, and how to remove data.
