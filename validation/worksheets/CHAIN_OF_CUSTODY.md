# Chain-of-Custody Form: Protocol OQ-P1

Use one form per **donor bone**, and a separate form per **graft lot** and per **data drive**. Every transfer of possession, location or state (frozen, thawed, fixed, filled, scanned, disposed) gets a row. Do not erase: strike through, initial and date any correction.
