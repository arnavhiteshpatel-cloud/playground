About

An early-stage research product

OsteoQuant builds analysis software for preclinical bone-graft studies. Its central idea is that a measurement tool should state which endpoints the data can support, and refuse the rest, rather than return a plausible number for everything.

How we work

Evidence rules we hold ourselves to

Every claim has a class and a source

Statements in our materials map to an evidence ledger that classifies each as a verified source, published finding, real-data result, synthetic result, vendor claim, assumption, hypothesis or unknown. Synthetic results may only be stated as “in simulation”. A claims checker compares numbers in the documents with the result files.

EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md · SCIENTIFIC_EVIDENCE.md

Criteria before data

Analysis conventions and acceptance criteria are registered before held-out data are analysed. Deviations are logged, including one where we could not verify that a policy had been fixed before an experiment, and withdrew that claim.

docs/science/PREREGISTRATION.md

Adversarial review cycles

Imaging, statistics, machine learning, architecture, biology, quality and security reviews are written down with severity, evidence and a response for each finding. Results that went against us stay in the record.

docs/reviews/cycle1/ · FORENSIC_AUDIT.md

Negative results we report

  • An explicit ROI-context input did not improve the network.
  • Synthetic-only models fail on real scans.
  • Our best morphometry agreement used a convention chosen after seeing the data.
  • The day-0 method degrades quickly with granule motion.
  • Analyst time saving has not been measured.

Context

What already exists

Established micro-CT packages and service providers already offer deep-learning segmentation, standard bone morphometry, reporting and, in some cases, audit and compliance tooling. We do not claim otherwise. Our focus is narrower: per-material separability assessment, abstention when graft and new bone cannot be told apart, a day-0 protocol for difficult materials, and audit-ready study reports for teams without dedicated imaging staff.

Our capability comparison, with an evidence tag and source for each cell: docs/research/COMPETITOR_MATRIX.md. Absence of a feature there means “not found in the sources reviewed”.
