Preclinical micro-CT · bone-graft and defect-healing studies

Micro-CT analysis that reports what it can support, and says when it cannot.

OsteoQuant is an offline desktop workstation for preclinical bone-defect studies. It separates new bone, residual graft and host bone where the material and the scan allow it, refuses to report endpoints it cannot support, records every correction in a hash-chained audit trail and writes study reports.

How measurement support works Validation status

What it does

Separate, abstain, record

01 · Separate

Three classes in the defect

Inside the defect region of interest, each mineralised voxel is labelled new bone, residual graft or host bone; voxels the method is unsure about are labelled uncertain. The default method is a classical, material-aware multi-Otsu split; locked models can be added.

Separation accuracy has so far been measured on synthetic phantoms only.

02 · Abstain

A measurement-support advisor

Before any number is reported, the advisor decides for each endpoint, material and scan whether it is supported, supported with conditions or not supported. Unsupported endpoints appear as “not measured”, with the reason.

Advisor version 1.4.2. Its decision rules are policy choices, to be revisited with real data. Support applies to the advisor’s own threshold method; with any other segmentation method, tissue endpoints are reported as not supported.

03 · Record

Audit trail and reports

Every import, analysis, correction and sign-off goes into an append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail. Corrections create new revisions with a stated reason; nothing is overwritten. DefectFill reports are written as PDF and CSV.

A technical control. It is not, by itself, Part 11 compliance.

Why abstention

A threshold always returns a number

Graft and new bone are both mineral. For many graft materials their X-ray attenuation overlaps, especially as the graft resorbs and the new bone matures. A threshold workflow still produces a residual-graft volume, and that volume can look plausible while being unidentifiable.

In simulation, once the graft density falls inside the overlap regime of Experiment C (800–1000 mgHA/cm³, within the assumed 650–1000 mgHA/cm³ range of woven new bone), the best possible intensity-only rule reaches a graft Dice of only 0.52–0.77, depending on noise. Synthetic

OsteoQuant therefore computes, before reporting, whether an endpoint is identifiable for the material and scan in front of it, and abstains otherwise. Total mineral volume, which does not need graft and new bone to be told apart, is reported whenever the scan supports it.

Safe, low coverage

Does the advisor refuse the measurements that fail? In the pre-registered held-out simulation S1H3 (advisor 1.4.1; 216 synthetic specimens; a failure is a volume error above 20%): residual graft volume supported in 37% of specimens, every failing measurement refused and none of the supported ones failed (95% upper bound on the supported-failure rate 0.045); supported graft volumes: median error 4.2%, maximum 13.0%; total mineral volume supported in 10% of specimens (sharp imaging only); supported mineral volumes: maximum error 3.2%; new-bone volume never supported. The shipped advisor 1.4.2 differs from 1.4.1 only in rules this test does not exercise. The simulator and the advisor share attenuation physics, so this is evidence of internal safety, not of accuracy on real scans; no physical validation has been run.

Workflow

From scan folder to signed report, on one computer

  1. ImportTXM, DICOM, NIfTI, NRRD, Zarr, TIFF and more. Voxel spacing must come from the file or be confirmed by you. SHA-256 provenance.
  2. Quality controlDeterministic checks (facts about the file) kept apart from heuristic checks (developer-chosen thresholds).
  3. AnalyseDefect ROI, segmentation and the measurement-support decision, as background jobs.
  4. CorrectBrush, region grow, fill, interpolate, undo. Saving creates a new revision with your reason.
  5. ReviewPassword re-entry to sign; authors cannot approve their own work; optional blinded mode.
  6. ReportDefectFill PDF and CSV listing every endpoint that was not measured and why.

Scope

What OsteoQuant is not

  • Not validated on real graft materials. Separation of graft from new bone has been tested only in simulation.
  • Not a medical device, and not intended for clinical use.
  • No GLP or 21 CFR Part 11 compliance is claimed, and no GLP study package is offered.
  • Not a cloud service: no telemetry, no accounts with us, no uploads.
  • Not a measured time saving. Analyst time has not yet been measured; the application can record it, locally and only if you switch it on.

Who it is designed for

Teams that study graft materials

Preclinical R&D groups at graft and orthobiologic manufacturers, study directors at contract research organisations, academic bone-repair laboratories and micro-CT core facilities.

OsteoQuant has no customers yet. We are looking for a small number of retrospective pilots in which the software is compared, blinded, with your own analysis under acceptance criteria written down before the data are seen.

Paid engagements start only after incorporation and IP clearance (no date set yet). Until then we can discuss free retrospective evaluations under a mutual NDA.
