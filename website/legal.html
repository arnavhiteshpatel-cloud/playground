Research-use notice

Research product — not validated on real graft materials; not a medical device; no GLP/Part 11 compliance claimed.

OsteoQuant software is intended for research analysis of preclinical (animal) micro-CT data. It is not intended for the diagnosis, treatment or prevention of disease in humans or animals. Automatic results must be checked by a qualified person before they are relied upon. Results labelled synthetic were obtained on computational phantoms and do not describe accuracy on real tissue.

Regulatory status

  • Medical devices. OsteoQuant is not a medical device and has not been cleared or approved by any regulator. Its status as a research tool has not been confirmed by a formal regulatory opinion.
  • GLP and 21 CFR Part 11. No compliance is claimed. The audit trail, electronic signatures and access controls are technical controls; compliance depends on a validated system, procedures and the user organisation’s quality system. Draft quality documents exist and are marked as drafts.
  • Use in regulated studies. Any use in a GLP study would require the study sponsor’s own computerised-system validation and quality assurance. OsteoQuant does not offer this.

No warranty

The software, documentation and this website are provided for evaluation “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Licence terms for the software have not yet been published; evaluation builds run in evaluation mode and watermark their reports. Pricing information on this site consists of indicative hypotheses and is not an offer.

Third-party software

The desktop application uses Qt through PySide6 (LGPL, dynamically linked; licence notices are shipped and relinking is possible), VTK (BSD) and the scientific Python stack (NumPy, SciPy, scikit-image, scikit-learn and others under permissive licences). Each build includes a THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/ folder with the licence texts of every bundled distribution. A software bill of materials is kept in docs/security/SBOM.csv.

Names and images

Product and company names mentioned on this site, such as Scanco IPL and Zeiss TXM, belong to their owners and are used only to describe file formats, reference software or comparisons. Their mention implies no endorsement, partnership or customer relationship; OsteoQuant has none.

All application screenshots on this site are real screenshots of OsteoQuant Desktop showing a demo project built from synthetic phantoms. The figures on the Science page are produced by the project’s experiment scripts. The site uses no stock photography.
