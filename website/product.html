Product

A desktop workstation for defect-fill micro-CT

OsteoQuant Desktop (version 0.1.0) is a Qt (PySide6) and VTK application that calls the analysis library in the same process. No server, browser or network connection is involved. A project is a folder on your computer holding the study database, imported scans, label revisions, reports and the audit trail.

Workstation

Import, check, view and correct

Import with provenance

A four-step wizard reads Zeiss TXM/TXRM, DICOM series, NIfTI, NRRD, Zarr, TIFF (multi-page or slice folders), PNG/BMP slice folders, MetaImage and NumPy files. Each scan is copied into the project with a SHA-256 hash; duplicates are detected.

Voxel spacing drives every physical measurement, so the wizard shows where it came from: file metadata, assumed by reader or missing. You cannot continue until every scan has explicit, confirmed spacing. Files that are not imported are listed with the reason.

Quality control, split honestly

Deterministic checks are facts about the file: voxel spacing, isotropy, missing slices, ingest errors, non-finite or saturated voxels. Heuristic checks are scores with developer-chosen thresholds, such as bone/soft-tissue contrast, ring and cupping indices, and anatomy truncated by the field of view. They flag scans for attention and are not established standards.

Each scan carries a verdict: suitable, review or unsuitable.

Viewer

Axial, coronal and sagittal slices with a linked crosshair, scale bars in µm or mm, window/level presets taken from each scan’s own histogram (grey values are uncalibrated unless an HA calibration is recorded), an oblique plane, and a VTK 3D panel with label surfaces and volume rendering. Light and dark themes.

Segmentation editing

Brush and eraser (2D disk or 3D sphere, radius in µm), region growing, hole filling and shape interpolation between edited slices. “Protect other classes” is on by default. Undo and redo are exact and keep the last 500 operations.

Saving creates a new corrected revision with your reason; the original is never overwritten, endpoints are recomputed with the same ROI, and per-class voxel changes go into the audit trail.

Measurement support

“Not measured” is a result

When you run an analysis you choose the graft material (or Unspecified, or No graft for empty-defect controls). The measurement-support advisor then decides, per endpoint, whether it is supported for that material and that scan, and by which mechanism.

The Support column in the Results tab summarises each scan: supported flagged not supported. An endpoint the advisor does not support is shown as not measured; hovering shows the reason, for example that graft and new bone cannot be separated for this material.

The desktop also flags results independently of the material: more than 10% uncertain voxels flags every endpoint and more than 25% withdraws support; Tb.Th below 2.5 voxels is flagged as partial-volume dominated; an automatically detected ROI requires review.

Audit and review

Every step attributed, every change kept

The audit trail is a technical control that records what happened and detects alteration. It does not by itself make a system compliant with 21 CFR Part 11 or GLP, and no such compliance is claimed.

Audit trail

  • Append-only: database triggers reject updates and deletes of audit rows.
  • Each row stores the hash of the previous row; Verify chain recomputes every SHA-256 hash and reports either an intact chain or the first altered event.
  • Every action is recorded under the signed-in user, with time (UTC), action, object and details.
  • Truncation of the newest events is detectable only against an externally recorded head hash (an anchor); keep one outside the project.

Users, roles and sign-off

  • Roles: administrator, analyst, reviewer, viewer. Passwords of at least 10 characters with an upper-case letter and a digit; five failed attempts lock the account for 15 minutes.
  • Approve, Needs changes or Reject require the meaning of the signature and password re-entry. The signature, its meaning and the hash of the signed labels are written to the trail.
  • An author cannot approve their own segmentation.
  • Blinded review mode hides group names, animal IDs and file names everywhere in the application, for every role.

Reports

DefectFill reports, with their exceptions

For a study, Generate DefectFill report builds a PDF and a CSV in the background: per-specimen results, representative orthogonal slices with the label overlay and ROI outline, QC verdicts and the hashes needed to trace each number back to its inputs.

Reports use the reported view of the metrics: endpoints the advisor does not support are printed as not measured and listed with their reasons; raw values stay in the audit record only. Reports can be previewed page by page, exported and signed by a reviewer.

Without a licence the application runs in evaluation mode with every function available, and reports carry the watermark “EVALUATION COPY – NOT FOR STUDY DECISIONS”. When a model trained on synthetic data was used for segmentation, the report carries a SYNTHETIC-TRAINED MODEL banner.

Platforms and formats

Where it runs today

Builds are PyInstaller one-folder packages. Nothing in a build needs a network connection at run time. Every installation can check itself with osteoquant-desktop-console --selftest, which runs import, QC, analysis, rendering, editing with undo, revision save, report and audit-chain verification, and prints PASS or FAIL per step.

Current limitations

What the application does not do yet

  • The day-0 (longitudinal) graft-footprint method exists in the analysis library but has no desktop workflow yet.
  • Material characterisation (measuring a new material’s intensity profile) is a library function, not a desktop feature.
  • Locked U-Net models are not offered in the default bundle: the ONNX runtime path still imports PyTorch. Classical multi-Otsu is the default segmentation.
  • The histology module is experimental and is not part of the desktop application or of reports.
  • CPU-only inference. A Generation-1 measurement gave about 49,000 voxels per second for the U-Net on 4 CPU cores (about 34 minutes per 100 million voxels).
  • Multi-gigabyte volumes: slab import and lazy display are unit-tested with small data and lowered thresholds, not with real multi-GB scans.
  • High-DPI displays, GPU drivers and multi-monitor set-ups have been exercised only through virtual displays.
  • The segmental-defect ROI detector can fail once healing bridges the gap; the scan is then marked needs ROI and you draw one.
